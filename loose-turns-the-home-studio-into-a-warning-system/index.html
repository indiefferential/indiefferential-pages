LOOSE Turns the Home Studio Into a Warning System

The devices that make a home convenient can also make it feel watched. On WHISPERING, LOOSE turns that unease into the subject of a rock song, tracing the presence of smart televisions, laptops, phones, and cameras through a world where privacy and free speech feel increasingly fragile. The track’s warning is direct, but its method is personal: Norbert Szabo, who releases music as LOOSE, recorded the single in an intimate home studio, performing every instrument himself before adding layers of lead and backing vocals.
