Two Birmingham Voices, One Answering Message: Matreya's "Truth"

What connects an artist first known as Mason Noise on The X Factor with world-renowned channeler, author and musician Lee Harris? In the case of "Truth," it began with a song, an Instagram message and an unexpected reply. Matreya had written a song that implores that there is nothing to believe in. He reached out to Harris, whose work had influenced his own journey of healing and self-discovery. Harris responded with words that became the answering voice within the record, asking whether Matreya could feel the future being built here and now. Adding another layer of synchronicity, both artists were born in Birmingham, despite taking very different paths into public life.
