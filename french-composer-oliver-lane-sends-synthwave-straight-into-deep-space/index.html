French Composer Oliver Lane Sends Synthwave Straight Into Deep Space

French composer Oliver Lane has never been interested in staying on the ground. His latest single, “Exodus,” was released on 29 October 2026, and it is an interstellar voyage that turns music into a science-fiction experience. Evolving electronic soundscapes suggest spacecraft arriving, departing and crossing distant galaxies, and the track carries a sense of mystery and immensity that rewards repeated listens. The track is built for listeners who want to be transported, not merely entertained.
