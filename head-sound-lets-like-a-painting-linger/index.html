Head Sound Lets Like a Painting Linger

A single guitar line is the thread that holds Head Sound’s Like a Painting in place, even as the song changes shape. The Albany-area shoegaze and dreampop band first introduced it as a demo on the 2022 Demos EP. It later became the most-saved song from the debut album, The Secret of Enjoying Life. Now Luxembourg producer Sun Glitters has extended that familiar center into a seven-and-a-half-minute rework, opening a longer view around the same line.
