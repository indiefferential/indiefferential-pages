Prem Byrne Makes Friends With the Mirror

It took decades to earn the message at the heart of 'Good Friend'. Prem Byrne's new single, released on all major streaming platforms on October 21, 2026, is a song about befriending the person in the mirror, and the independent singer-songwriter says its words have been settling into his bones for a long time. The authenticity behind them, he explains, was hard won.
