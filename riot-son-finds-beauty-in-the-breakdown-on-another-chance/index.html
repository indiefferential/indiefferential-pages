RIOT SON Finds Beauty in the Breakdown on “Another Chance”

Somewhere between a sun-faded summer memory and the restless ache of a relationship falling apart, RIOT SON finds a sound that feels both nostalgic and unmistakably unsettled. Released on October 20, 2026, “Another Chance” captures the North Carolina artist’s distinctive blend of guitar-driven alternative rock, surf-rock shimmer and early-2000s garage-rock grit, turning romantic uncertainty into something raw, melodic and emotionally immediate.
