She's Wild and Choosing Herself: Little Quirks' Bolder New Chapter

Australian indie-rock outfit Little Quirks return with "Game Boy," out September 18th, the latest taste of their forthcoming album She's Wild. The track marks another step into a darker, bolder new chapter for the New South Wales band, following the release of the album's title track and "Moving On." Punchy, playful and packed with attitude, "Game Boy" takes aim at the smooth-talking romantic who seems to have perfected the art of making everyone feel like the only one, flowers appearing at just the right moment, spontaneous dancing breaking out in the rain, every line timed just a little too perfectly, until the whole performance starts to feel rehearsed. It is playful without being lightweight, catchy without losing its bite, carrying the kind of restless energy that makes it impossible to sit still.
