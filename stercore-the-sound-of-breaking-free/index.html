STERCORE: The Sound of Breaking Free

STERCORE’s “TO LOVE TO HATE” turns pressure into a collision of sound. Modern metalcore aggression meets electronic elements and the kinetic charge of Drum and Bass, while the song’s subject is the struggle to be accepted without hiding essential parts of the self. Inspired by the pressure of growing up in Central Europe, it confronts homophobia, racism, discrimination and the demands of fitting in. The result is framed not as a detached commentary, but as a heavy, energetic anthem for outsiders and anyone unwilling to disappear behind other people’s expectations.
