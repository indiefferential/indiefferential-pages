OpCritical Wants You Off the Couch

Most protest songs ask listeners to care. OpCritical asks them to move. The band's new single, 'Act Up', released on October 16, 2026, is a punk-charged kick in the backside aimed at anyone who has watched the world's troubles from a comfortable seat and waited for somebody else to deal with them.
