Fifty Something and Just Getting Started: One Artist's Late, Loud Arrival

Aformer dancer and garden builder, 50mething arrived at a simple question at age fifty-eight: with a large back catalogue of completed, unreleased tracks sitting untouched, was this finally the time to get involved in the music business? The answer, delivered with characteristic directness, was: why not. Pronounced "fifty something," the artist name captures both the age and the attitude behind a genuinely late-blooming project, one shaped as much by life experience as by musical ambition.
