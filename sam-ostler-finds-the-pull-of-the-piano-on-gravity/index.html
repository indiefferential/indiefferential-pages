Sam Ostler Finds the Pull of the Piano on ‘Gravity’

Sam Ostler builds songs around a familiar instrument and an expansive feeling. A pianist and singer-songwriter, he brings soulful keys, heartfelt writing and a broad pop frame together without losing the intimacy at the center. His latest single, “Gravity,” released on November 6, 2026, places that balance in focus: emotional in its premise, polished in its scale, and shaped to rise into an anthemic refrain. The title suggests a force that draws people together or keeps them close to what matters, a fitting image for a songwriter whose stated aim is connection.
