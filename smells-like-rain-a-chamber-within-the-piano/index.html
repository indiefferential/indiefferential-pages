SMELLS LIKE RAIN: A Chamber Within the Piano

Smells Like Rain’s “Crystals in the Catacombs (Instrumental)” makes a first statement through contrast. Dark, brooding rhythm sections move against soaring, lyrical strings and sparkling high-register electric guitar, creating a piece that occupies the space between jazz and post-rock. Released November 13, 2026, the single also marks a significant threshold for the Austin-based artist: a first deep dive into mixing and producing. The track is not simply a composition presented for listening, but an account of a sound taking shape through deliberate choices in performance and recording.
