Lessons From the Classroom: Rosie Byron's Anthem for Everyday Joy

Rosie Byron has spent her working life in two classrooms at once, one filled with children learning to read, the other filled with listeners learning to be kinder to themselves. A pop singer-songwriter, primary school teacher and proud mother of six, Byron builds music rooted in authenticity, hope and the quiet beauty of everyday life, writing songs that encourage self-love, resilience and finding light even in life's most challenging moments.
