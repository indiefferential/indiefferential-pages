Baby Booder Turns Late-Night Pressure into ‘Whatever You Wanna Call It’

Baby Booder’s debut album begins in a place that is specific enough to become universal: the neighborhood where he grew up, the booth after a difficult day, and the moment when a feeling needs somewhere to go. “Whatever You Wanna Call It,” due November 27, 2026, was recorded in Southeast Area Park in Ace Deuce, Michigan, alongside sessions in Milan, Michigan and Miami, Florida. Its spontaneous freestyle sessions and late-night recording process preserve the urgency of emotions arriving before they can be neatly explained.
