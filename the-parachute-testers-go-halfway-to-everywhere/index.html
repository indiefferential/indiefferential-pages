The Parachute Testers Go Halfway to Everywhere

Some debut albums announce themselves with a manifesto. The Parachute Testers announce theirs with a view. Halfway to Everywhere, the first album from the genre-blurring collective, was recorded overlooking the Irish Sea at Black Mountain Studios in County Louth, and it arrives on October 30, 2026. The setting seems to have seeped into the music, which has the spaciousness of a place where land, water and sky share the same horizon.
