How to Be in the Snow: Steven Luke's Cello-Driven Reckoning With Seasons

Independent singer-songwriter and musician Steven Luke has announced the upcoming release of his highly anticipated new single, "Seasons," dropping on 1st October 2026. Melding atmospheric guitar work with a powerful, anthemic classic rock energy, the track offers a deeply vulnerable exploration of human resilience, connection and what it truly means to weather life's inevitable transitions, using the cyclical shifting of the year to map out the highs and lows of the human experience.
