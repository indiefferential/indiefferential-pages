Letting the Waters Rise: The Mountain's Cinematic Leap of Faith

The Mountain is an independent musical force from Belfast, built around the haunting vocal presence of Rebecca Dow and the cinematic production mastery of Darren Speers. Described as pure spiritual synchronicity, the partnership brings together two distinct artistic voices from Ireland's alternative and indie spheres: Dow, a recognised figure known for her mystical depth and absorbing lyricism, and Speers, who draws on an extensive background composing film scores and crafting intricate sound-worlds. After a considered search for the perfect collaborator, Speers found in Dow an artist whose genuine self creates pure magic within their shared work, and together they build atmospheric, immersive tapestries that feel deeply rooted in Irish soil while remaining boundless in scope.
