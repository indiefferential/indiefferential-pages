Spy Score Meets Boom-Bap: Don Cicci Remixes "James Bond"

Some samples come with their own cinema attached. On the "James Bond" remix single, released on 10th April 2026, London producer Don Cicci takes a piece by Italian composer Enzo Minuti and adds beats that capture the action-packed energy of the iconic spy film franchise. The track is described as a masterful example of his signature sound, a fusion in which dramatic score meets urban boom-bap. Detailed production changes and dynamic arrangements keep the listener engaged, so the sample never settles into a simple loop.
