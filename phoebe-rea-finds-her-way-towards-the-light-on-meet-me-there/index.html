Phoebe Rea Finds Her Way Towards the Light on “Meet Me There”

Grief has a way of changing the landscape of everyday life. A familiar view, a favourite café, or an unexpected moment of beauty can suddenly become a reminder of someone who is no longer there. For Oxfordshire-born, London-based singer-songwriter Phoebe Rea, these small encounters form the emotional heart of “Meet Me There,” a piano-led new single that transforms personal loss into a celebration of connection, memory, and the things loved ones leave behind.
