JC Flow: A Story That Isn't Finished

Some artists make music to be heard. JC Flow makes music to be believed. The Seattle hip-hop artist, songwriter and producer has built a catalogue around resilience, honesty and transformation, and his newest single, 'Still Becoming', released on October 16, 2026, may be the clearest statement of that mission yet.
