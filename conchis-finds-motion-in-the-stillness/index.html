Conchis Finds Motion in the Stillness

The first movement of Gravity happened without sound. During a prolonged period of severe illness, Conchis could not make music in the ordinary physical sense, so the Helsinki artist composed an album inwardly: chords, rhythms, lyrics, and arrangements imagined in silence and darkness before they could be recorded. That unusual beginning is not a dramatic footnote attached to the work.
