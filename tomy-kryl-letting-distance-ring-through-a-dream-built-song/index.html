Tomy Kryl: Letting Distance Ring Through a Dream-Built Song

Tomy Kryl’s “Neutíkám” began with a distance that could not be solved by arrangement: a period of separation from someone he loved. The Czech-language single turns that absence into an intimate expression of attachment, using the image of two birds on his partner’s tattoo as a visual counterpart to the song’s emotional bond. The artwork and music share a quiet purpose.
