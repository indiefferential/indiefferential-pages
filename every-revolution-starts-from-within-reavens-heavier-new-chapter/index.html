Every Revolution Starts From Within: Reaven's Heavier New Chapter

&quot;Reavolution" marks a powerful new chapter for Reaven, revealing a heavier, more intense and unapologetically modern rock-driven side of the band. Bigger, louder and more visceral than what came before, the album combines raw energy, powerful melodies and anthemic choruses with a clear desire to break boundaries. More than simply an album, Reavolution is framed as a state of mind, a journey through change, freedom and transformation, driven by the belief that every revolution starts from within.
