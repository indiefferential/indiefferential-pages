Mental Gymnast Finds New Angles in Synthetic Hardcore

Mental Gymnast have been a band since early 2026, but the members came to the project with years of experience already behind them. The group has moved quickly, reaching a third EP without suggesting any intention to slow down. That early momentum suits a band whose sound is built around sharp contrasts and restless ideas. Its defining proposition is concise: hardcore with synths in place of bro-coded elements, a shift that changes the texture without surrendering punk’s directness.
