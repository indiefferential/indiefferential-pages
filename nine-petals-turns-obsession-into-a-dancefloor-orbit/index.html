Nine Petals Turns Obsession Into a Dancefloor Orbit

In Miami, where a tiny downtown studio becomes a laboratory for sound, Nine Petals follows ideas wherever they lead. Reggae tones and Latin color form part of the artist’s musical vocabulary, but no single genre sets the borders. The recent turn toward techno began with an encounter that lingered: over two days, five people described being consumed by obsessive love. One was devoted to someone unaware of their existence. The observation carried a troubling persistence, and it called for a musical language capable of circling without relief.
