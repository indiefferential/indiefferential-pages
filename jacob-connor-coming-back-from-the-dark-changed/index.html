Jacob Connor: Coming Back from the Dark, Changed

“Back Here” begins with the aftermath of a struggle. Jacob Connor’s country-rock single looks at what happens when someone emerges from hardship and recognizes that the fight has changed the person who survived it. Its central image is a mirror: a familiar face carrying marks that were not visible while the battle was happening.
