NOANNE Opens the Door to Obsession and Illusion in Cinematic Noir “Suite 17”

Some songs tell stories. Others create entire worlds, inviting listeners into places where memory, desire, and reality become difficult to distinguish. With “Suite 17,” Prague-based artist NOANNE delivers an atmospheric psychological noir that feels less like a conventional single and more like the soundtrack to a mysterious film yet to be discovered. Featuring acclaimed producer, musician, and singer-songwriter Gregory Darling, the track explores obsession, emotional dependency, and the seductive danger of returning to places that should have been left behind.
