Sergiy Bolotny’s Polka, Recast for the Virtuoso Violin

The violin in Sergiy Bolotny’s Ukrainian Caprice No. 11 carries a dance impulse into the concert tradition. Subtitled Homage to Ukrainian Polka, the solo work is described as spirited and joyful, drawing on the bounce and celebratory spirit of traditional Ukrainian polka rhythms. Its energy offers a deliberate contrast to Reflections: Oh, Human Destiny, the earlier war-themed elegy centered on the tragedy of war in Ukraine. Here, Bolotny turns toward life-affirming motion.
