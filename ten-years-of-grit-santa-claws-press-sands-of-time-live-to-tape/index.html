Ten Years of Grit: Santa Claws Press Sands of Time Live to Tape

It took ten years of passion, resilience and transformation for Brittany's Santa Claws to arrive at the sound that defines them today. That sound is on full display on Sands of Time, the Alt-Rock and Britpop quintet's second album, released this October. Self-produced in the band's own studio in La Chapelle-Neuve, mixed in-house by Antoine Montgaudon and mastered to analog tape by Christophe Chavanon at the legendary Studio Kerwax, the record blends raw 1990s rock energy with rich, melodic songwriting, and it captures what the band calls its full artistic maturity.
