Nerisah Find Beauty in the Imperfect and Haunting on “East Ghost”

some songs begin with a melody; others emerge from memories that refuse to remain in the past. For Paris-based duo Nerisah, music offers a way to follow those memories into unexpected places, blending raw guitars, shifting vocals and unconventional sounds into something that resists easy definition. Their new single, “East Ghost”, draws on the personal history of Bosnian-born vocalist Mersiha, exploring the lingering presence of war through a haunting, atmospheric arrangement that balances melancholy with a distinctly human sense of discovery.
