DEAU EYES: The Quiet Architecture of Becoming

A record made over several years can hold more than one version of its maker. For Deau Eyes, To Grow Instead is described as a musical time capsule: a gathering of experiences, challenges and transformations that shaped its creation. The album arrived November 13 through Richmond-based Blank Verse Records, marking the Richmond artist’s first full-length release in four years and her most personal and expansive work to date. This marks a significant artistic evolution. Instead of presenting growth as a straight line, its premise makes room for the seasons and shifts that a long process can contain.
