Echoes Across Erased Timelines: Memory, Reincarnation, and Archival Plunderphonics

Emerging from an elusive background with shifting accounts of an upbringing spanning Bangalore, London, and lost geographies, contemporary electronic producer DARSHAN.404 operates on the intriguing borderland between sonic memory and creative invention. Central to the mystery of his artistic identity is his connection to an obscure trove of unidentified recordings recovered during a Berlin audio restoration initiative between 2004 and 2009.
