Sweet Mess: The Last Thing Rock Refuses to Forget

A name can disappear from conversation long before the body that carried it is forgotten. Sweet Mess build the song around that unsettling idea, the so-called second death: the moment a person is remembered for the last time. Rather than treating mortality as an abstract puzzle, the Phoenix hard-rock band turns the concept into a demand for connection across time.
