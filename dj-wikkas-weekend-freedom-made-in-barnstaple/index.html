DJ Wikka's Weekend Freedom, Made in Barnstaple

Monday to Friday can feel like a long wait for the music to start. DJ Wikka's new single, 'Saturday Night', makes the wait worthwhile. Released on 27 October 2026 through Wikka Beats, the track turns the weekday grind into weekend freedom, blending bright synths, a grooving bassline and catchy pop melodies with an energetic club drop. Its chorus celebrates letting go under neon lights, with a simple refrain about weekend freedom that is built for singing along.
