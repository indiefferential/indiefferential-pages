PINK JAGG: A Joke Turns Into a Hurricane

Pink Jagg’s “Hurricane” begins with a small provocation between brothers and arrives somewhere more exposed. Written and recorded at Selina Music Studios in Medellín, Colombia, while Austin Spacy was touring with Karol G, the song reportedly grew from a lighthearted jab about a girl between the brothers into one of the duo’s most vulnerable pieces. That change of temperature is a compact portrait of Pink Jagg itself: a project shaped by brotherhood, rap, visual imagination and an inward search for identity that complicates the surface of a personal story.
