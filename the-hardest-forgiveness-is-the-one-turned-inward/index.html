The Hardest Forgiveness Is the One Turned Inward

A piano note can leave enough room for a whole history to arrive. In Forgiven for the Last Time, Exzenya uses that room to turn a familiar breakup narrative toward a less comfortable subject: the person left behind is not the only one seeking release. The song’s emotional pivot is self-forgiveness, approached without softening the regret that comes before it.
