Sarah McGuinness Celebrates a Decade of Christmas Music with the Live-Recorded Christmas Everyday

Christmas has a way of bringing every emotion to the surface. Joy and nostalgia sit alongside longing and heartbreak, while familiar melodies can transport listeners back to people, places and moments that remain close to the heart. For Sarah McGuinness, that emotional richness has been at the centre of a decade-long festive musical journey. Now, marking ten years since the release of her debut Christmas single, the independent singer-songwriter, producer and performer celebrates with *Christmas Everyday*, a sparkling five-track mini LP recorded live at London's Dean Street Studios.
