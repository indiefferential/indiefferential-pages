Arabella Lucia Turns "Unpretty" Into a Defiant Rock Anthem for a Filtered Generation

Beauty standards have always had a way of changing shape, but the pressure to conform has rarely disappeared. For Arabella Lucia, that tension provides the starting point for a bold reimagining of TLC's 1999 classic "Unpretty", transforming a song about appearance and self worth into a guitar driven statement for a generation navigating filters, FaceTune and the constant judgement of algorithmic culture.
