Tillerman Put Reputation on Trial in a Britpop Whodunnit From West Yorkshire

Accusations spread fast in the social media age, and Tillerman have turned that idea into a bright, hook-filled pop song. Their latest single, “Reputation,” released on 28 October 2026, explores how quickly a claim can spiral out of control. It is an indie-pop track with big choruses and infectious melodies, rooted in 1990s Britpop with splashes of classic 1960s and 1970s pop-rock, and its theme is reminiscent of Stereophonics’ “A Thousand Trees.”
