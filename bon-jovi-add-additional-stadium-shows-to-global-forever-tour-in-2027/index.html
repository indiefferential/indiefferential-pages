Bon Jovi Add Additional Stadium Shows to Global Forever Tour in 2027

Iconic Rock band Bon Jovi has announced seven additional dates for their Forever Tour, which includes shows at stadiums across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia. The announcement comes on the heels of recent successful tours such as a nine-show residency at Madison Square Garden in New York City earlier this year and three stadium shows in August and September in the U.K. Bon Jovi’s lead singer Jon Bon Jovi emphasized how vital these shows have been to their audience's support, saying, ‘The Forever Tour has been an incredible celebration of the music and the fans who have been with us all these years.’

 He highlighted the special energy shared with audiences, noting 'the thousands of faces singing along with us, night after night.' The new dates for 2027 include shows in Melbourne, Sydney, London, East Rutherford, Chicago, Foxborough, Toronto, and more. Fans can now register for tickets to these North American venues starting on October 28th at 10 AM local time, with additional presales set to take place throughout the week prior to a general release on October 30th at 10 AM.
