Cat and Mouse in Sound: Nick Norton-Smith Scores a Tabloid Takedown

Some film scores support the story; others behave like a character inside it. For One Rogue Reporter, the satirical documentary from director Rich Peppiatt, composer Nick Norton-Smith built something firmly in the second camp. The original soundtrack, now officially released, mixes live instrumentation with sharp, modern electronic textures to mirror what the film itself plays like: a thrilling, high-stakes game of cat and mouse in which a former tabloid journalist turns the tables on newspaper bosses through mischievous stunts and interviews with prominent public figures.
