Allegra Turns the Dancefloor into a Confessional

Allegra’s pop has always understood the dancefloor as a place for feeling, not an escape from it. The UK singer and songwriter moves between club-ready singles and diaristic writing, letting bright production carry stories about heartbreak, independence and self-discovery. Her recent “Unreleased EP” collects five tracks that show her increasingly assured voice: sleek, infectious pop on the surface, with candid lyricism underneath. The contrast is not a contradiction. It is the way these songs turn private turbulence into something that can move a room.
