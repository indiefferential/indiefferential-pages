Aperture Finds a Room Inside the Music

A single opening in a roof can decide where light falls across an entire room. Suat Soyyiğit builds Aperture around that architectural image, turning a small feature of a place into the guiding idea for an instrumental composition. The track belongs to A Structure for Falling Light, an album of seven pieces, each written about a location that stayed with the Istanbul-based architect and composer.
