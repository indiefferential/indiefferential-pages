Weight and Repetition: The House Flies Dig Into Lions and Ore

Moline, Illinois has a way of seeping into a record, and The House Flies make no attempt to scrub it out. The post-punk and dark alternative band releases its second full-length album, Lions and Ore, on Friday, November 6, 2026, through Wilhelm & Sons Entertainment Company. Seven tracks arrive on Bandcamp and streaming platforms, with a limited run of 100 black 12-inch vinyl copies for those who like their gloom in physical form.
