Nicole Mish and the Restless Hope of Talking to Ghosts

The ghost in Nicole Mish's song is not the sort that rattles chains. In 'I Talk To Ghosts', the singer wanders out at night, leaving behind the attachment to modern shiny things in order to spend time with ghosts: real people, past and present, who are beautifully flawed and complicated. Eerie, mid-tempo and reverb-soaked, with banjo and spooky guitars, it suits Halloween season without turning ghoulish.
