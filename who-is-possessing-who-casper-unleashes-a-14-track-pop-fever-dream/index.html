Who Is Possessing Who? CASPER Unleashes a 14-Track Pop Fever Dream

When you create a larger-than-life version of yourself, what happens when that character starts taking over? That question sits at the center of POSSESSION, the new 14-track album from independent South Carolina pop artist CASPER, due October 6, 2026. It blends futuristic electro-pop, Y2K club energy, rock attitude and hip-hop influence into what he calls a deliberately chaotic world, and it explores obsession, fame, nightlife, sexuality, insecurity and the blurred line between CASPER the character and the person behind him.
