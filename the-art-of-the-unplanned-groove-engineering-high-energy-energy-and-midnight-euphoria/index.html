The Art of the Unplanned Groove: Engineering High-Energy Energy and Midnight Euphoria

Singer-songwriter, producer, sound engineer, and multi-instrumentalist Shahaf Wieder, performing under the moniker Golsie, brings a distinct blend of analytical precision and spontaneous creativity to contemporary indie rock. Grounded in a professional background in computer engineering, the frontman of Golsie & TheWaves approaches sound design and songcraft with technical mastery while maintaining an open, curious exploration of visual media and technology.
