Sloppy Joe's Give a Live Favourite New Life on "Right Decision (Remastered)"

Some songs earn their place in a band's catalogue long before they receive an official spotlight. They become part of the live experience, familiar to audiences who know exactly when to sing along and how to respond when the opening bars ring out. For German rock band Sloppy Joe's, "Right Decision (Remastered)" brings one such favourite back into focus, giving a long standing crowd pleaser a refreshed recording without losing the raw energy that made it a fixture of their shows.
