Miss Wednesday Makes Protest Feel Like an Invitation

Miss Wednesday’s answer to the climate crisis begins with an acoustic guitar and ends with a room full of people singing along. The Norwegian artist’s eco-folk-pop project uses catchy melodies, pointed lyrics, and a warm performance style to make protest feel welcoming without making its subject seem small. Foreboding Tales and Egotistical Butterflies: Part I gathers songs that move through anger, consolation, sorrow, and hope, drawing a line between the urgency of environmental concern and the possibility of collective action.
