Brandes Develops a Life from the Fading Frames of ‘Old Photographs’

A photograph fades, but the people inside it do not disappear from the life they helped shape. “Old Photographs,” the third single from Brandes’ forthcoming second album “Still Life,” begins with that quiet recognition. The UK singer-songwriter looks back through memory for snapshots that have grown fogged at the edges, tracing how relationships and encounters remain present even as their details change. The song’s premise is intimate, but its invitation is broad: nearly everyone has an album of such images stored somewhere.
