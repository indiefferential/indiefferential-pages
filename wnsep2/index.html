We just wanted to say thank you for the opportunity to work together. At Indiefferential Magazine, we’re truly grateful to connect with artists like you who bring passion, honesty, and creativity into the world.

We strongly believe in the power of physical media and in the bright future artists can build in today’s digital landscape. This is more than just another online feature.

Indiefferential exists to document independent music and give artists a lasting place where their work can be discovered long after a release day has passed.
