The Layman Preachers Let the Partner Tell the Other Side

Everybody knows the fantasy: leave the dull life behind, become reckless, and start over as someone more exciting. The Layman Preachers take that familiar daydream and give it a second voice. In the song at the center of their latest description, one character imagines torching an ordinary life, while a partner patiently takes the fantasy apart line by line.
