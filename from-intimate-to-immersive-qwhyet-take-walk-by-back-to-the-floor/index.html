From Intimate to Immersive: Qwhyet Take 'Walk By' Back to the Floor

Los Angeles electronic duo Qwhyet return to "WALK BY" with a new Club Version that pushes the track's emotional vocal-house foundation into a harder, more immersive dancefloor setting. Originally released as part of the duo's HUSH EP, "WALK BY" introduced one side of Qwhyet's sound: hazy, lo-fi house textures, intimate vocals and an understated emotional pull. The Club Version takes those same elements and gives them considerably more room to move, increasing the track's physical presence while keeping the vocal and underlying feeling firmly at its center.
