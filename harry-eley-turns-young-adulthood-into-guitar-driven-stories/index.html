Harry Eley Turns Young Adulthood Into Guitar-Driven Stories

Harry Eley writes from inside the unsettled terrain of young adulthood: relationships shifting, identity taking shape, and private struggles unfolding beneath the polished surfaces of social feeds. Based in Guildford, Surrey, the singer-songwriter and guitarist turns those pressures into reflective songs built around melodic hooks, expressive guitar work, and an emotional register that can feel intimate without becoming small.
