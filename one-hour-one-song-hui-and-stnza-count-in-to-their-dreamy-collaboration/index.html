One Hour, One Song: Hui. and STNZA Count In to Their Dreamy Collaboration

Sometimes a song arrives in an hour and stays for years. "Count In," the new collaborative single from Melbourne artists Hui. and STNZA, is out on October 9, with cover art by Hugh Davies and Ethan Sheils. A dreamy blend of art rock, soul and jazz, it offers the first glimpse of the upcoming EP All That I Need, due November 13, and explores themes of hope, life and longing.
