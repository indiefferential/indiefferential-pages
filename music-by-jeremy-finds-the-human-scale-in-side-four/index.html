Music by Jeremy Finds the Human Scale in ‘Side Four’

Music by Jeremy closes a year-long, 24-track autobiographical project by looking not for a grand finale, but for the detail that makes a life feel lived. “Side Four,” the last chapter of “Scenes from a Well Worn Path,” arrives on November 25 with six songs about ageing, appetite, money, memory, passion and legacy. Its emotional range moves from a fear of obsolescence in “Dinosaur” to a tender Barcelona weekend in “Vincci Bitten,” then to the monthly arithmetic of “Slow in Fast Out.” Dry observation and private feeling share the same modest frame.
