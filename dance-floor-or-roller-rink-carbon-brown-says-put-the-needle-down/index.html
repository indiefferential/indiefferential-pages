Dance Floor or Roller Rink: Carbon Brown Says Put The Needle Down

Possibly one of the most danceable tunes you will hear today, "Put The Needle Down" by Carbon Brown is built around an earworm of a hook designed to have listeners spinning on the dance floor or the roller rink. Beneath the infectious surface, though, the lyrics carry a deeper inner meaning, offering the listener motivation to take on the world. It is a track that works on two levels at once: a physical pull toward movement and an emotional nudge toward resolve.
