Melisa Mia: Womanhood, Recast as a Three-Act Soundtrack

Photography by @simonnewburyphotography Melisa Mia builds the self-produced concept album around three phases of womanhood represented by the triple-goddess archetype, moving from the shame and vulnerability associated with maidenhood toward generational memory and, finally, the reclamation of the crone.
