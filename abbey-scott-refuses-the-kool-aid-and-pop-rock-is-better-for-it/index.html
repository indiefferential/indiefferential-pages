Abbey Scott Refuses the Kool-Aid, and Pop-Rock Is Better for It

Los Angeles singer-songwriter Abbey Scott has never been interested in blending into the crowd, and her new single makes that clear from the first chorus. “Kool-Aid,” released on 15 May 2026, is a bold, high-energy pop-rock anthem that urges listeners to think for themselves in an increasingly divided world. Its message is not tied to any party or political camp. Instead, it focuses on unity, authenticity and the value of questioning outside influences rather than following the herd. The track works as a reminder that a catchy chorus can carry a serious point without sounding like a lecture.
