Daddy Drwg Finds the Fairy-Tale Fine Print in ‘Life’s Not Disney’

The clockwork robot is battered, the glass slipper has been replaced with a toilet brush, and the fairy tale has aged into an aching back and dirty fingernails. On “Life’s Not Disney,” Welsh alternative artist Daddy Drwg approaches adulthood through dark comedy, turning shattered expectations and everyday absurdity into a song that refuses to mistake cynicism for wisdom. Released November 27, 2026, the single takes familiar storybook comfort and asks what happens when the promised magic meets the ordinary mess of being alive.
