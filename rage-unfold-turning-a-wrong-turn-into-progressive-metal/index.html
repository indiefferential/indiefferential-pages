Rage Unfold: Turning a Wrong Turn into Progressive Metal

Rage Unfold’s “Dislocated” makes uncertainty audible before the lyrics need to explain it. The progressive-metal track begins at half tempo, holding back its full force, then shifts into its normal pace. That structural change turns the song’s theme into a compositional event: the feeling of standing at an uncertain crossroads becomes a movement from suspended time into acceleration.
