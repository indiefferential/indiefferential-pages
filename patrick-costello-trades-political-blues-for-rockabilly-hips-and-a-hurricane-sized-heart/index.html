Patrick Costello Trades Political Blues for Rockabilly Hips and a Hurricane-Sized Heart

For much of his career, Patrick Costello has written songs that carry weight, from political blues rock to emotional bluegrass. With “Heart Like a Hurricane,” released on 26 October 2026, the San Francisco-based artist turns toward fun, sexy and danceable territory for the first time. It is a bold change of gear, and the single delivers an electric rockabilly explosion built for the dance floor. It is the kind of record that asks a room to move before it asks a listener to think.
