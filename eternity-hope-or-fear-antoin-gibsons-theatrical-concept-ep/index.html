Eternity, Hope or Fear: Antoin Gibson's Theatrical Concept EP

What happens when the end of life is not necessarily the end of existence? That is the question at the center of The Rise and Fall of Eternity, an EP released on 9 October 2026 and described by its creator, Antoin Gibson, as their most ambitious conceptual project to date. It is a theatrical, genre-defying journey through identity, humanity's self-destruction, mortality and the unknowable nature of consciousness.
