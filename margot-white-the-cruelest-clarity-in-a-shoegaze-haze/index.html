Margot White: The Cruelest Clarity in a Shoegaze Haze

Margot White’s “Cruelest Pill” sits at the instant when illusion gives way to clarity. The London-based songwriter and producer shapes that change as a meeting between devastation and liberation, a moment when a painful truth can alter the way someone understands both a relationship and the self. Ethereal vocals float above expansive, distortion-soaked production, while a recurring refrain gives the track a memorable center.
