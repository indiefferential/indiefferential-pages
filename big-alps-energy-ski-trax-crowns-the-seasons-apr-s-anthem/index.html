Big Alps Energy: Ski Trax Crowns the Season's Après Anthem

There is a particular window of time on a ski holiday: the last run is done, the boots are heavy, the sun is hitting the ridge, and the bar is calling. Ski Trax has built a project around that window. The snow-obsessed producer is turning the global après-ski scene into dancefloor gold, and the latest offering, "I'm Just Here For The Après," is pitched as the official 2026/27 ski-season anthem, produced by Gareth Shortland.
