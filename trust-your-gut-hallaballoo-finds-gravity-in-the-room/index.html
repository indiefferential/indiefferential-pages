Trust Your Gut: Hallaballoo Finds Gravity in the Room

For the drummer, the song was little more than question marks on charts and trust in each other. That is how Travis Johnston remembers the creation of "Ain't No Big Wup," one of nine tracks on Gravity, the new album from Minneapolis collective Hallaballoo, released October 2, 2026. The song came out of sessions with almost no pre-production, and by then, Johnston says, the musicians had spent enough time together to try things that probably would not have happened in their first year.
