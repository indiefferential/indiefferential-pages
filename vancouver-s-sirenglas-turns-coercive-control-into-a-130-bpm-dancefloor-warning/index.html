Vancouver’s Sirenglas Turns Coercive Control Into a 130 BPM Dancefloor Warning

Vancouver’s Sirenglas has found a way to make a disturbing question move the body. The dark electronic project, the work of Vancouver-based artist Alfred Hermida, has released Still You Move, a hypnotic EBM single that explores the psychology of coercive control and the point at which obedience begins to feel like choice. Its relentless pulse and icy textures are designed for the darker corners of the dancefloor, but the message beneath them is what lingers after the final beat. The single is a strong example of how the project turns darkness into rhythm.
