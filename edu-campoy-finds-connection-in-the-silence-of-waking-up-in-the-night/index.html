Edu Campoy Finds Connection in the Silence of Waking Up in the Night

Seville songwriter Edu Campoy has spent a lot of time in the dark, and he has come out with a six-track EP to show for it. On 9 October 2026, he released Waking Up in the Night, a record that treats darkness not as something to escape but as a place to explore. As V.E.N!, the solo project he leads, Campoy blends melodic power pop and alternative rock with progressive elements and atmospheric textures, and the result sounds energetic and introspective at once.
