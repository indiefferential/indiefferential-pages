Love That Stays: Elias Dream's "Forever by Your Side"

Some love songs celebrate the beginning of something. "Forever by Your Side," the new song from Elias Dream, is about the kind of love that stays, through fear, through pain, and through the moments when words are no longer enough. It is a deeply emotional, cinematic pop and soft-rock song in which storytelling comes first, and listeners are meant to do more than hear it. They are invited to feel the story behind it.
