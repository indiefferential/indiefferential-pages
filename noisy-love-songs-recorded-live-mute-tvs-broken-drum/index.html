Noisy Love Songs, Recorded Live: MUTE TV's "Broken Drum"

Some bands polish their sound until the edges disappear. MUTE TV prefer to keep the edges. The South West trio's new single, "Broken Drum," arrives on 2 October via Medical Grade Music, and it was recorded live at The Heavy's studio within Peter Gabriel's Real World Studios. The track captures the energy and chemistry of three musicians playing together in a room, with nothing tidied away.
