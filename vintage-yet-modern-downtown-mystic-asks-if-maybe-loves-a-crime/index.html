Vintage Yet Modern: DownTown Mystic Asks If Maybe Love's A Crime

Rock'n'roll is spoken here, and DownTown Mystic is fluent. The project, the alter ego of American rocker Robert Allen, returns with a new single, "Maybe Love's A Crime," released worldwide to all streaming services by The Orchard/Sony Music on October 9, after the success of the Mystic Highway Road Trip EP. The new single is described as a surprising departure from what reviewers might expect, and it drops at US college radio on October 13, promoted by Brandon Day of Tinderbox Music.
