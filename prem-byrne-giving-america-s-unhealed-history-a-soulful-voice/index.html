Prem Byrne: Giving America’s Unhealed History a Soulful Voice

Prem Byrne’s “Here In America” approaches history through the lens of the people who continue to live with its consequences. The single addresses the wounds caused by slavery in the United States, not as a distant subject sealed in textbooks but as an injury whose effects remain visible in the present. Byrne’s writing began with his study of U.S. history and his observations of poor neighborhoods, where questions about justice and the legacy of slavery became impossible to keep abstract.
