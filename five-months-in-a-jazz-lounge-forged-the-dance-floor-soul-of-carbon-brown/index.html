Five Months in a Jazz Lounge Forged the Dance-Floor Soul of Carbon Brown

Some bands are built in rehearsal rooms. Carbon Brown was built on a bandstand. The Northwest Arkansas quartet spent five months as the house band at a local jazz and R&B lounge in 2026, and the experience sharpened a sound the group describes as indie pop soul, with clear nods to danceable, R&B, funk and jazz. The result is a band that can fill a room with groove and still leave space for intricate harmonies and sharp individual playing.
