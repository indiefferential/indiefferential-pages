Orshina Turns Grief Into a Louder Kind of Presence

Grief can change the scale at which a sound needs to speak. Orshina’s Graveyard Blues – Artist Vision revisits a composition from Requiem for Nova with greater force, putting the artist’s guitar presence at the center and reflecting a desire to express the music more freely. Recorded in a personal studio, the single combines a vintage aesthetic with contemporary production, but its most significant change is one of emphasis: the composition is no longer contained by the earlier album version.
