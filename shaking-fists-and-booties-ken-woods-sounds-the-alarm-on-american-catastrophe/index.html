Shaking Fists and Booties: Ken Woods Sounds the Alarm on American Catastrophe

Protest music has always had to choose between making people think and making them move. Ken Woods and the Electrick Reckoning want both. Their new single, "S.O.S.," is described as a searing and hilarious indictment of a presidency they see as sleepwalking toward an American catastrophe, and it is meant to get listeners shaking both their fists and their booties. The song's refrain, "You promised us change, and this sure isn't it," sets the tone.
