Same Story, Two Versions: Spinors Cage the Listener in a Heartbreak Riddle

Heartbreak rarely has just one version. London steampunk alternative rock band Spinors build their latest single, "YOU LOVED THE CAGE," around that idea, presenting a breakup in which, depending on the listener's point of view, the story can be read in two different ways. The song and its video each tell a different version of the same events, leaving the audience to decide whose account to believe.
