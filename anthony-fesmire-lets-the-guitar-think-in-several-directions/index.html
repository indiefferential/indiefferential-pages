Anthony Fesmire Lets the Guitar Think in Several Directions

The opening idea of The Quiet Road is not a lyric but a conversation between guitar, piano, and rhythm section. Composed by Anthony Fesmire, the medium-tempo instrumental brings together jazz-rock writing and strong R&B influence, with Scott Smith on piano and keyboards and David Lockeretz and David Oromaner anchoring bass and drums.
