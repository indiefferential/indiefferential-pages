HVNDS Put Guilt Under Pressure on ‘Control’

On “Control,” HVNDS make heaviness feel unstable in the most compelling sense. The Romanian band’s third full-length album, released through Radikal Records, moves among modern metalcore riffs, nu-metal grooves and industrial electronics, building a sound that can lurch from a blooming synth harmony into a wall of guitars. Its central subject is guilt and the possibility that it cannot simply be outrun. Rather than treating that weight as an abstract theme, the band makes it audible in abrupt contrasts, dense low end and choruses designed to hit with collective force.
