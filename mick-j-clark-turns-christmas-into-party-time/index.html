Mick J. Clark Turns Christmas Into Party Time

A Christmas party is the whole premise, and Mick J. Clark’s seasonal song is built around a straightforward invitation to celebrate. Its subject needs no elaborate mythology: Christmas brings people together, and a party supplies the setting. In a crowded holiday season, that directness can be its own point of view. The song turns toward communal cheer, making the occasion rather than a complicated plot its center of gravity.
