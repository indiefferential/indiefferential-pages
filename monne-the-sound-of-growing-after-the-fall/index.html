MONNE: The Sound of Growing After the Fall

MONNE’s first double-album installment inhabits the uncertain stretch after a life has come apart but before a new shape is visible. Loss, unrest, hope and transformation all belong to this in-between space, where the old order no longer fits and the next one has not yet taken form. The Norwegian singer-songwriter and producer does not treat hardship as a simple prelude to triumph.
