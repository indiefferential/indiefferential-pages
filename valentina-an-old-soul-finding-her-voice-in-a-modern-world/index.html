Valentina: An Old Soul Finding Her Voice in a Modern World

Some voices seem to carry stories long before the first lyric begins. South London singer-songwriter Valentina brings that quality to her music, blending soul, R&B, jazz and classical influences into a sound shaped by emotional honesty and an unmistakably old-soul sensibility. With Amy Winehouse, Adele and Raye among her inspirations, she draws from a rich musical heritage while searching for a contemporary identity that feels entirely her own.
