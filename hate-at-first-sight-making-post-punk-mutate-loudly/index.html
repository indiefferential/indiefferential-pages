Hate At First Sight: Making Post-Punk Mutate Loudly

Hate At First Sight does not treat musical lineage as a set of rules to obey. The Reno project brings post-punk’s angular instincts, hardcore’s pressure and alternative rock’s restless invention into a sound that can be aggressive without surrendering melody. “Familiar Mutations,” the project’s June 2026 EP, captures that balance through forceful guitars, melodic hooks, dynamic structures, bass lines and 1980s synth textures.
