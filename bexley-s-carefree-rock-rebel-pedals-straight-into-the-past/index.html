Bexley’s Carefree Rock Rebel Pedals Straight Into the Past

Some artists chase the future. Aaron Lewis Moore has spent his latest work looking back, and it sounds all the better for it. Hailing from Bexley, England, the singer has built a sound that folds together the raw energy of Guns N’ Roses, Nirvana, Metallica and Oasis, filtered through a vocal style that owes a clear debt to Axl Rose. The result is classic rock with modern grit, delivered with a carefree spirit that feels like a deliberate counterpoint to a scene obsessed with polish.
