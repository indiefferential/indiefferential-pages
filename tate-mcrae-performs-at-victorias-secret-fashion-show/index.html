Tate McRae Performs at Victoria's Secret Fashion Show

At this year's Victoria's Secret Fashion Show, held on October 18th, Tate McRae performed new single 'Housewife' along with two other hits during the event. She showcased her performance in a bright red lingerie set, complete with matching backup dancers and a group of angels who wore angel wings as she performed. 

The singer also sang 'Dear God' from her album So Close to What, featuring an ultra-polished dance break with herself at its center, before closing out with the Billboard Hot 100 hit 'Sports Car.' The event is part of Victoria's Secret’s return to a live format after previously holding pre-taped versions of the show for six years. McRae was joined by Megan Moroney and KATSEYE provided additional music during the fashion show.
