Michael Waters Writes Melodies Built to Stay With You

Michael Waters writes from a simple conviction: a memorable melody can carry a feeling farther than any production trick. The UK-based songwriter’s catalogue moves through adult contemporary, soft rock, pop and indie balladry, pairing accessible hooks with stories that can be reflective, romantic or openly uplifting. He writes both music and lyrics, shaping songs meant to remain in the listener’s mind and, as his own description puts it, work as karaoke-friendly choices. The phrase is playful, but it points toward a serious craft: a chorus has to invite people in.
