Hägerstrand, the Minimalist Who Shared a Stage With Nirvana

Peter Hägerstrand has spent most of his life making music, and now he has decided to strip it back to what matters. Sjögräs Vol. 3 arrives as the next part of a project the Finland-based artist describes as a kind of coming of age, a distillation of everything he does best. He removed everything that did not need to be there and kept only the essentials.
