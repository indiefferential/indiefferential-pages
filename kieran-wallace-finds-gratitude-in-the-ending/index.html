Kieran Wallace Finds Gratitude in the Ending

Breakup songs usually pick a side: grief, anger or relief. Kieran Wallace's new single, 'Incredible', takes the longer view. Dated September 28 for release on all major streaming platforms, the track reflects on love, separation and acceptance from a place of distance, tracing the way a relationship's end can move from questioning why it had to happen to gratitude that it happened at all.
