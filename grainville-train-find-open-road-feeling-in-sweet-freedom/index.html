Grainville Train Find Open Road Feeling in ‘Sweet Freedom’

“Sweet Freedom” opens a window onto a gentler side of Grainville Train without asking the Finnish band to surrender its rock spirit. The song follows two people leaving everyday routines behind, choosing love and the present moment over the obligations waiting elsewhere. A warm soft-rock sound and a 1970s-inspired feel give it a natural road-trip current. Under the melody sits the group’s familiar attitude: freedom is tender here, but it is not weightless.
