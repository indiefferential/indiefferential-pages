A Farewell to Summer, NYC Style: Nia Marie's "when the summer ends"

There is something almost palpable about the wind-down that happens when the seasons change in New York City. It is the feeling of knowing that something is shifting, paired with a quiet question about what the next season might hold. Queens-based Pop/R&B singer-songwriter Nia Marie captures that in-between feeling on "when the summer ends," a dreamy, soulful, relationship-coded farewell to summer released September 18.
