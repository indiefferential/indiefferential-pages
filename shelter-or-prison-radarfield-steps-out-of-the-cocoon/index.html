Shelter or Prison? RADARFIELD Steps Out of the "Cocoon"

Acocoon sounds like safety: somewhere to hide from the outside world, disappear and wait until everything has changed. But what if the cocoon is no longer protecting you, and has become the thing keeping you inside? That unsettling question drives "Cocoon," the latest chapter of Berlin darkwave act RADARFIELD, and perhaps its most intimate one.
