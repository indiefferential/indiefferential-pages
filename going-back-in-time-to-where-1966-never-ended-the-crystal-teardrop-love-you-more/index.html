Going Back in Time to Where 1966 Never Ended: The Crystal Teardrop Love You More

In a world full of AI, auto-tune and generic-sounding music, The Crystal Teardrop are going back in time to show how they wish it was. The London band, whose blend of garage rock, psychedelia and acid folk springs from a shared passion for the sights, sounds and creative experimentation of the mid to late 1960s, return with LOVE YOU MORE, an infectious second album on Popclaw, the subsidiary of Rise Above Records. It arrives barely a year after their debut, The Crystal Teardrop Is Forming, which came out on the same label in May 2025.
