CASH L3WIS Plays the Ocarina of Time in Hip-Hop

Video games rarely get the hip-hop treatment they deserve, which makes CASH L3WIS's next move worth noticing. The independent artist releases 'Ocarina of Time' on October 30, 2026, through his label ESOT3RIK, featuring Wiz Khalifa and produced by DopeBoyzMuzic. The record takes its name and its mood from one of the most celebrated video games of all time.
