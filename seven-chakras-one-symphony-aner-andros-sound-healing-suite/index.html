Seven Chakras, One Symphony: Aner Andros' Sound Healing Suite

Sound has long been used as a tool for calm, and Italian composer, multi-instrumentalist and sound healing practitioner Aner Andros has built an entire work around that idea. The Chakra Symphony is an immersive instrumental piece inspired by the seven chakras and by the relationship between sound, frequency and inner balance. It is structured across seven movements plus a closing coda, each composition connected to a specific chakra and tonal center, and it blends meditative soundscapes, acoustic instrumentation and contemporary composition into a deeply atmospheric listening experience.
