A Frequency, Not Just a Sound: Flo Frequency's Restorative Return to Center

Soul and contemporary R&B artist Flo Frequency has officially released Infinite Light, a full-length, sixteen-track album produced, arranged and curated by veteran London-based producer-DJ Nav Bhatt. Built as a restorative sanctuary in an era defined by detached minimalism, the record offers a deep, head-nodding sonic environment centered on emotional healing, romantic arrival and inner clarity, working from a guiding philosophy that music isn't simply something to listen to, but an atmosphere to live inside.
