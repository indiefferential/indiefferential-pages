Built From the Basement Up: Chet Nordy's Love Loss

Hailing from Freeland, Michigan, Chet Nordy, the performance name of Dave Nordstrom, is a multi-talented artist who transforms heartfelt emotion into powerful rock anthems. With the vision and determination to bring his musical ideas fully to life, Nordstrom designed and built Nordy Studios in his own basement, creating a professional recording space where he writes, composes and records all of his material. That environment, meticulously built from the ground up, has become an intimate creative space where his artistry is free to flourish on its own terms.
