Frequencies of Fatherhood: How Analog Synthesizers and Tape Loops Crafted a Sanctuary of Quiet Reflection

Transitioning away from fifteen years of writing and performing traditional folk music, Brighton-born and Leon-based artist Lewis Mckale has embarked on a deeply serene sonic evolution under the reflective moniker AKALM, an acronym standing for Also Known As Lewis Mckale. Following the birth of his daughter a year ago, Mckale made a conscious decision to pause live gigging and step away from louder musical formats.
