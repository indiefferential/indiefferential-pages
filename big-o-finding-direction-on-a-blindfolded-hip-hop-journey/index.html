Big O: Finding Direction on a Blindfolded Hip-Hop Journey

“Blindfolded” takes its title from a condition many people recognize: having to move through life without knowing what is waiting ahead. The hip-hop EP turns that uncertainty into a cinematic journey, pairing San Diego rapper Benny Canales with American London-based producer Big O. Its themes move through nostalgia, isolation and conflict toward the possibility of freedom, while refusing to pretend that hope requires a clear map.
