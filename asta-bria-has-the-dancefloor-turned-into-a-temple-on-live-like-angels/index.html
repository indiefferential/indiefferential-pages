Asta Bria has the Dancefloor turned Into a Temple on “Live Like Angels”

What if the dancefloor could be more than an escape? For Asta Bria, it can become a place of transformation, where rhythm takes on the power of ritual and a repeated lyric becomes something close to a prayer. On “Live Like Angels,” the international artist brings that philosophy into focus, blending hypnotic vocal chants with deep, rumbling electronic production to create a sound that reaches beyond the usual boundaries of club music. It is a bold continuation of the style she calls “chant dance,” and a track with ambitions that extend well beyond the dancefloor.
