Shelia Moore-Piper Brings Faith Into the Groove

Shelia Moore-Piper’s music is built to lift the spirit and move the body at the same time. On Let’s Celebrate, energetic soul meets live instrumentation, funky bass lines, prominent horns, and a soulful vocal presence. The song’s invitation is direct: celebration can be a physical act, a shared groove, and a way of making optimism audible.
