A Nomad Finds a Home in Song as Peter Stone’s Hearth Takes Shape

After a lifetime spent searching for home, Peter Stone has built one out of songs. The nomadic artist’s second album, Hearth, is due on 13 November, and it arrives as a self-produced collection of quietly ferocious alt-folk recorded in New York City, the current base for a life that once stretched across cities and continents. The record is a piece of home, welcoming anyone who chooses to listen. Its songs were written on the road and built to make listeners feel at ease.
