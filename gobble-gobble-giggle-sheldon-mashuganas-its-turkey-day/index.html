Gobble, Gobble, Giggle: Sheldon Mashugana's "It's Turkey Day"

Holiday songs usually reach for sentiment. Sheldon Mashugana reaches for a turkey. "It's Turkey Day," released on 13th October 2026, is an upbeat, comedic anthem built for the holiday season, and it tells the hilarious story of Sheldon and Crystal chasing after a turkey on Thanksgiving Day. The chase includes a visit to a Dallas Cowboys game, and the lyrics are liberally sprinkled with fart jokes throughout, a promise of silliness that the title makes clear from the start.
