Skar de Line Builds an Epic Ending From the Inside Out

Save Me is designed as an ending that changes what came before it. The final track in Skar de Line’s five-song arc, it brings the suite begun with 2025’s Vignette toward a large, anthemic close, calling back to motifs from earlier songs and giving them a new context. The track arrives November 13, 2026, with a cinematic video planned for November 20.
