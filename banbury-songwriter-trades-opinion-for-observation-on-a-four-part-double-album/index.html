Banbury Songwriter Trades Opinion for Observation on a Four-Part Double Album

Jeremy has made a debut that refuses to behave like one. The Banbury singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist has set out to create a 24-track, four-part double album that works as a musical autobiography told through key scenes. The third instalment, Scenes From a Well Worn Path (Side Three), is due on 25 October 2026, and it turns the project’s gaze outward, moving from inner reckoning toward broader tensions. The project asks listeners to hear a life in episodes, and Side Three is where those episodes begin to speak to the wider world.
