Stolen Valor: Tony Frissore and AXIOM Reckon With Service and Meaning

Some records exist because certain things are difficult to say in ordinary conversation. That is precisely why Tony Frissore and collaborator AXIOM made "Stolen Valor." Both men have spent significant parts of their lives connected to military service and the national security community, an experience that gave them friendships, purpose, opportunities and an identity neither takes lightly. It also makes it difficult, they have said, when what they see happening around that institution conflicts with what they believed their service represented. AXIOM put that conflict into words, and Frissore built the music around them.
