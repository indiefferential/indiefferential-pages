Jack Maria Makes the Uncanny Feel Intimate

Blank does not offer a clean way out of its own atmosphere. Jack Maria’s album moves through hopelessness, numbness, trauma, and the uneasy experience of no longer recognizing oneself, settling into a progression the Toronto-area artist describes as increasingly negative. That direction is not hidden by the production; it is built into it.
