No Smoke, No Mirrors: O.W.T.O. Press Record on Real Music

In an era of polished perfection, O.W.T.O. offers something deliberately old-fashioned: real musicians with real instruments getting into a studio and pressing record. No smoke, no mirrors, no autotune and no machines. It is all live. Under the creative guidance of Dr P.A. Francis, the project seeks to remind its audience why authenticity still matters, with a musical pulse somewhere between nostalgia and evolution. It describes itself as a large G&T: Grooves and Tunes.
