Snowapple Collective Takes Cyborg Art Pop to the Dancefloor

Snowapple Collective closes a chapter of its story by sending it to the club. The international collective returns with remixes of two fan favorites from its 2025 album Utopia, My Body and She Ella, reimagined by producer Alessandro Baldessari and released on Friday 18 September. The pitch is simple and a little cheeky: Return to Utopia, but this time with a beat you can move to.
