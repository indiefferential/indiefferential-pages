Be My Wife: Glamour, Heartbreak and Bad Taste Confess

the project lives in the unstable space where a confession can become a performance before anyone has decided which one it is. The Paris-based project is the musical alter ego of creative director Federico Nessi, who has spent more than two decades performing, making images and refusing to leave music alone. A moment in the spotlight in 2023 became a personal turning point, leading to a project described as somewhere between a nervous breakdown and a cabaret act.
