Sarah Smith Turns Inner Conflict Into Dark Pop Drama on “Point of No Return”

Desire can be as unsettling as it is intoxicating, particularly when the longing to be loved collides with an instinct to push intimacy away. On “Point of No Return”, New York-based pop artist Sarah Smith transforms that contradiction into dark, cinematic electro-pop, exploring the anxious space between wanting to be chosen and resisting the vulnerability that comes with being seen. Her second single pairs pulsing production with emotionally charged songwriting, capturing a narrator caught in a cycle of desire, uncertainty and self-sabotage.
