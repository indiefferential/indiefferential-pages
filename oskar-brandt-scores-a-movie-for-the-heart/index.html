Oskar Brandt Scores a Movie for the Heart

Every breakup leaves behind a question, and the cruelest version is the one that targets the past itself: was any of it real? Oskar Brandt builds a whole song around that doubt. 'Imagination' is a cinematic orchestral pop track that turns post-breakup uncertainty into sweeping, emotionally charged storytelling, and its haunting central question asks whether the love and the relationship were genuine, or whether it all existed only in his mind.
