A Sadboi With Soulful Melodies: STRlighT's Ever Lasting Heart-Healing

STRlighT describes himself simply as "just a sadboi with soulful melodies," a modest tagline for an artist who has become one of the most notable figures in the Chinese Melodic EDM scene. As a music producer, STRlighT brings his sincere musical thoughts to the world through unique insights into melodies, atmosphere and arrangement, consistently emphasizing authentic emotion and aesthetic feeling over flashier production trends, with an underlying hope of healing the hearts of every listener who encounters his songs.
