Nineteen Releases In: Ronald Hage's Handmade Approach to Dance Music

Consistency is its own kind of statement, and few independent electronic producers demonstrate that quite as clearly as Ronald Hage. A dedicated melodic house and progressive house producer from The Hague in the Netherlands, Hage has spent several years crafting dancefloor-ready tracks, and his new single, "Don't Wanna Lose This," marks his nineteenth release after eighteen months of consistent output, a testament to both his prolific work rate and his commitment to releasing new material roughly every four to six weeks.
