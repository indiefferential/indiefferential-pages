D.D.R. Hits the Low-Battery Warning on ‘All Outta’

“All Outta” gives a recognizable modern condition the scale of an arena-rock emergency: the social battery has run dry, and somebody still has one more question. Released November 11, 2026, the single turns depleted patience into a melodic hard-rock singalong, complete with gang shouts, stacked backing vocals and a throwback guitar solo. D.D.R.’s joke lands because the frustration underneath it is real. The song offers a loud answer to the small daily demands that can make a person feel officially unavailable.
