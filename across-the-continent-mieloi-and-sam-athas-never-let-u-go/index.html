Across the Continent: Mieloi and Sam Atha's "Never Let u go"

Some collaborations begin in a studio. This one began on a trip. "Never Let u go," released on 9th October 2026, is a collaboration between producer Mieloi and Sam Atha, built from vocals that Atha initially recorded during a visit to Portugal. Atha's self-recorded vocals and an initial beat concept provided the foundation, and Mieloi then developed and expanded the idea in his bedroom studio in Leuven, Belgium. The cross-continental creative process resulted in a track that balances atmospheric liquid elements with the raw intensity of jungle rhythms.
