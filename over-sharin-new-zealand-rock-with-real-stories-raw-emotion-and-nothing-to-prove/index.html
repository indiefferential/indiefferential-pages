Over Sharin: New Zealand Rock with Real Stories, Raw Emotion and Nothing to Prove

There is something refreshing about a band that has no interest in chasing the next trend. For Tauranga-based alternative-rock project Over Sharin, the priority is simpler and considerably more enduring: write good songs, tell honest stories and make music that means something. Driven by emotive songwriting, atmospheric guitar work and a willingness to explore uncomfortable subjects, the New Zealand outfit is building a catalogue that values substance over spectacle.
