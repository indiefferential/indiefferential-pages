UCHAYE: Heartbreak, Hendrix and a Bedroom Studio

UCHAYE has turned a breakup into a five-act war movie, and she has scored it herself. The Minneapolis-based Nigerian-Zimbabwean artist releases her debut EP, WAR IS OV3R, on 23 October 2026, a concept record that follows one protagonist through the aftermath of a relationship, from obsessive denial to escape, then to a hardened pursuit of power and finally to full, electric rebirth.
