The Industrial Craft of Libre Post-Punk: Noise, Agit-Prop, and Open-Source Ideology

Formed in early 2007 by principal creator Graham alongside a fluid rotation of creative collaborators, Vancouver-based project The Silence Industry operates as a raw, uncompromising poured expression of independent sound. Rejecting music as a commercial entertainment commodity, the project views songcraft as direct communication, noise, personal journaling, love letters, and political agit-prop, releasing works under open Creative Commons licensing for listeners to freely share.
