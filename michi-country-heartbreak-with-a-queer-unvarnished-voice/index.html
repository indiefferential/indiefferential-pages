Michi: Country Heartbreak with a Queer, Unvarnished Voice

“Whiskey” begins with one of country music’s oldest questions and changes who gets to ask it. After a relationship lasting more than a year ended in the discovery of infidelity, Michi wrote a song about sitting with a drink, replaying memories and wondering whether the person who left might be thinking about her too. The heartbreak is familiar; the perspective is not always given room in country music.
