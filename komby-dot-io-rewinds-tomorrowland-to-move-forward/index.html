KOMBY DOT IO Rewinds Tomorrowland to Move Forward

A festival memory can become a musical blueprint. For KOMBY DOT IO, attending the inaugural Tomorrowland in Belgium in 2006 left a lasting connection to electronic music, one that resurfaces in Tomorrowland Never End, a five-minute progressive-house anthem shaped by the euphoric energy of EDM’s golden era and the perspective of two decades in music.
