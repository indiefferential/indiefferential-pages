Reinhout Finds Light Inside the Mist of ‘What Remains’

Reinhout’s music begins with a feeling and lets the rhythm arrive later. The Oslo-based producer, also known as Stijn Reinhout Firmin Viaene, has built an introspective electronic identity distinct from the sunlit soundscapes he releases as Firmin. Under the Reinhout name, the atmosphere turns toward colder seasons, inward attention and the emotional afterimage of moments that have passed. His album “What Remains,” released November 13, 2026, gathers those concerns into a carefully sequenced journey between distance and connection, nostalgia and hope.
