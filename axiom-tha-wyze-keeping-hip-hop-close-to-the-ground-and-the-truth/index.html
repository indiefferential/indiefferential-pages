Axiom Tha Wyze: Keeping Hip-Hop Close to the Ground and the Truth

A chance meeting on tour becomes a studio collaboration in “Pay Attention,” the new single from Boise rapper and producer Axiom Tha Wyze and Planet Asia. Their connection began on the road, where a shared respect for authentic lyricism and hip-hop’s foundations turned quickly into creative chemistry. The resulting track brings two distinct voices into the same frame without asking either to dilute what makes them recognizable.
