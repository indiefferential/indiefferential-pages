Coffey’s “Feel It All”: Finding Light on the Other Side of Grief

Grief can turn even the brightest moments into something distant, leaving life moving forward while the heart struggles to catch up. For Los Angeles-born Chinese-American singer-songwriter Coffey, “Feel It All,” released on October 15, 2026, transforms that emotional disconnection into a vibrant synth-pop statement about rediscovering feeling, embracing vulnerability and learning to navigate life without the person who once provided its compass.
