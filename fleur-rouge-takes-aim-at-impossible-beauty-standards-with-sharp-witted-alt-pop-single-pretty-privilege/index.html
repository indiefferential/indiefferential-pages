FLEUR ROUGE Takes Aim at Impossible Beauty Standards with Sharp-Witted Alt-Pop Single “Pretty Privilege”

Aging is inevitable, but apparently accepting it has become increasingly optional. In a culture saturated with filters, cosmetic enhancements, and relentless demands for youthful perfection, women are often expected to remain forever desirable while society quietly moves the goalposts. London-born, French-raised alt-pop artist FLEUR ROUGE tackles this contradiction head-on with “Pretty Privilege,” a sharp, satirical new single that turns the absurdity of modern beauty standards into an energetic pop statement.
