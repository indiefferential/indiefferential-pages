Honest by Design: DARNELL Turns a Hard-Won Life Into Freedom

Some artists arrive at honesty as a style choice. For DARNELL, it reads more like survival instinct. The London-based singer, songwriter and producer builds music rooted in honesty, vulnerability and emotional accountability, drawing directly from real experiences of love, regret, resilience, relationships and personal growth, less interested in presenting a polished version of himself than in documenting what actually happens when life doesn't follow the plan.
