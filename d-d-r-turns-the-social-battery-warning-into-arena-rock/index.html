D.D.R. Turns the Social Battery Warning into Arena Rock

D.D.R. understands that exhaustion can have a chorus. On All Outta, the songwriter Stephen Paul turns a depleted social battery into melodic hard rock with enough swagger to make retreat sound like a communal event. Released on November 11, 2026, the single takes the familiar feeling of having nothing left in the tank and gives it a full-volume outlet. Its humor is pointed but not detached: fed-up patience becomes the very material from which a singalong can be built.
