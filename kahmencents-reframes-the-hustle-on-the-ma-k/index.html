KahMenCents Reframes the Hustle on ‘The Maćk’

KahMenCents does not reject the word “hustle”; he changes what it can mean. On “The Maćk,” the Tulsa-based artist revisits the work ethic he once saw in street hustlers and describes a moment of clarity: the same persistence could be redirected toward building rather than harming a community. Released in March 2026, the single frames transformation as a practical change in where ambition points. Its soulful production and introspective writing turn a personal reorientation into a message of possibility.
