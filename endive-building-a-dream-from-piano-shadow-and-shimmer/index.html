endive: Building a Dream from Piano, Shadow and Shimmer

endive’s debut EP “headprint” moves between dream pop, ambient pop and melancholic art rock, using atmosphere as a way to approach experiences that resist a simple narrative. Love, longing, coming of age, self-discovery, self-destruction and identity circulate through the record, often in a space where dreams and waking life overlap.
