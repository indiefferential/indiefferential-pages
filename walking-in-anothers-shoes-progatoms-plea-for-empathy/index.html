Walking in Another's Shoes: ProgAtom's Plea for Empathy

Online arguments tend to start with certainty and end with cruelty. Norwegian progressive rock band ProgAtom answers that pattern with "New Shoes," a single tackling online trolling, hateful discourse and the importance of empathy in the digital age. Released today, it is described as a powerful artistic statement, combining complex musical arrangements with socially conscious lyricism. The poignant lyrics ask listeners to consider perspectives beyond their own, with a line like "I haven't been in your shoes, so who am I to judge?" serving as a reminder of shared humanity.
