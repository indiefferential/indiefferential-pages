Electric Elm: Knowing When a Song Needs Room to Breathe

Electric Elm is built around a creative principle that places the song ahead of the people making it. Singer-songwriter Chris Bousquet, who also records as American Elm, and multi-instrumentalist and producer Eric Lichter are longtime friends from neighboring Connecticut shoreline towns. Their shared interests range across folk, country, rock, folk-rock, country-rock and power pop, with no strict boundary around where a good song can come from.
