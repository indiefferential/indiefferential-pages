Joe Nilson: Who Can Make a Heart Begin Again?

“Quién será?” begins with the vulnerability of someone considering whether love can return after emotional pain. The title asks who might be capable of bringing affection and peace back into a life, and Joe Nilson builds the song around the uncertainty inside that question. Rather than present romance as a guaranteed remedy, the Dominican singer-songwriter focuses on the hesitation that comes before opening up again: the mixture of longing, caution and hope that follows a difficult experience.
