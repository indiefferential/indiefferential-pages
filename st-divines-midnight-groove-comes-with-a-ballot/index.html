St. Divine's Midnight Groove Comes With a Ballot

Rock and roll has a long tradition of telling people what to do, and St. Divine keeps that tradition alive with a message of three syllables: Please, vote. The New York punk and Americana band releases 'Midnight (to Track)' on October 16, 2026, a song that was recorded during the sessions for its debut album and then cut from the vinyl for timing reasons. The band decided it needed to be heard now.
