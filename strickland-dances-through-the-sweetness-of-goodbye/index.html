Strickland Dances Through the Sweetness of Goodbye

Dandelion turns the end of a relationship into something bright enough to move to. Strickland describes the new single as an upbeat account of the hopelessness that can follow a breakup: love begins with the fragile promise of a flower held in the hand, then disappears with the wind. The song does not ask listeners to pretend that loss feels good.
