Haunted by a Misremembered Past: Firestations Return to Full Band Form

London art-pop band Firestations, who describe themselves as a dreampop folkgaze group on Lost Map Records, are back with International Dust, a nine-track album due in October 2026. It marks a return to their full five-piece format after last year's more stripped-back alt-folk journey on Many White Horses. The lineup is Mike Cranny on vocals and guitar, Laura Copsey on vocals, keys, flute and flugelhorn, Martin Thompson on guitar, synths and piano, Tom Hargreaves on drums and percussion, and Neil Walsh on bass, viola and vocals.
