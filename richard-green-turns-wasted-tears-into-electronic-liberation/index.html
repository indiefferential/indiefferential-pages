Richard Green Turns Wasted Tears Into Electronic Liberation

Heartbreak has a strange way of revealing what truly matters, and on “Wasted Tears,” Italian musician and composer Richard Green transforms that realisation into an atmospheric electronic statement. Released on January 7, 2026, the indie-dance single explores the emotional energy spent on people and situations that ultimately prove unworthy of such investment. Beneath its melodic hooks and shadowy textures lies a universal coming-of-age lesson: growing older means learning which battles deserve the tears.
