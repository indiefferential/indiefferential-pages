MICHAEL & THE GUILLOTINE Introduce False Prophets to the Hydra

Some alternative rock songs plead for attention. 'The Hydra Blues' demands it. The new single from MICHAEL & THE GUILLOTINE, released on October 23, is a bluesy, gritty, guitar-driven track about survival, about fighting for your peace and about calling out the false prophets and fake messiahs who think they own the truth.
