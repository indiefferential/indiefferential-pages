A Son’s Devotion Brings Private Family Recordings Into the Light

Some records carry a story that matters as much as the music, and this instrumental release is one of them. Krey Galin serves as the creative vehicle for a personal musical legacy that stretches across generations. The compositions were created years ago by Gregory Allen, a seasoned producer with roots in the 1990s and 2000s, and it was his son’s dedication and love for the music that finally brought these recordings to the public. The idea of a family archive finally reaching listeners gives the project a rare sense of purpose.
