The Heat Inc. Find the Melody Inside the Noise

The Heat Inc. make distortion and melody feel less like opposing forces than partners in a productive argument. Dead Pony Club, a new single from the band, takes its place ahead of a forthcoming album due in March 2027. Abrasive guitars and an infectious hook supply the immediate impact, while an emotionally resonant lyric gives the noise somewhere to lead. The track’s central tension is clear: weight and urgency press against a chorus designed to stay, with neither side cancelling the other.
