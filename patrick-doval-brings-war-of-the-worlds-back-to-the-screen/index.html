Patrick Doval Brings War of the Worlds Back to the Screen

Patrick Doval approaches rock as both a song form and a cinema screen. The Miami-based independent singer-songwriter and recording artist pairs emotion-driven writing with energetic arrangements and visual storytelling, building a body of work that moves between sound and image. His latest single, War of the Worlds, turns toward a familiar science-fiction premise, then makes the accompanying video a space for his own interpretation of an alien invasion story.
