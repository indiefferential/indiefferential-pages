July Serves Up a Taste of Late-Night Temptation on “WINGS”

Nightlife has its own language: flashing lights, fleeting encounters and decisions that seem considerably better after midnight. On “WINGS,” London-based independent R&B artist July embraces that world, exploring desire, temptation and the indulgent pleasures of club culture. Built around a recurring “lemon pepper wings” hook, the single gives its after-dark atmosphere an unexpected lyrical signature, pairing contemporary R&B influences with a confident, hedonistic attitude.
