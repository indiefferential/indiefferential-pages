Nachtwacht: Making the Small Hours Feel Infinite

Nachtwacht’s “Perpetual Beings” resists the conventional rush toward a dramatic peak. Built layer by layer in Ableton, the track lets distance, repetition and fleeting emotion gather slowly, as if each listen reveals another outline in a room that never fully explains itself. The Liverpool duo’s music is designed for liminal hours, when the day’s demands recede and time seems to lose its usual shape.
