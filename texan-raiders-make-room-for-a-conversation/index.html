Texan Raiders Make Room for a Conversation

The central image of Log Settee is modest: a place to sit, talk, and give another person more than a passing glance. For Texan Raiders, the song becomes a defense of genuine connection in a time when communication can be reduced to social-media exchanges. Its story is rooted in a collaboration with songwriter Gavin Alexander at a Chris Difford songwriting retreat in Italy, but its subject is familiar far beyond that setting: the value of spending time with people who matter.
