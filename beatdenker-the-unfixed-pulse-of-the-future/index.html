BEATDENKER: The Unfixed Pulse of the Future

Beatdenker begins with a question that sounds like a dare: how far can rhythmic complexity travel toward the dance floor without giving up its unruly nature? For Jo Wespel, the Berlin-based guitarist, composer, producer and rhythm explorer behind the name, the answer is not a compromise but a widening of possibility. His music treats pulse as a living material, forever shifting shape while keeping the body in forward motion. The forthcoming album, arriving November 20, 2026, takes that proposition into a playful, high-energy statement born from a challenge by longtime collaborator KID BE KID: make the music as danceable as possible, while keeping its singular musical language intact.
