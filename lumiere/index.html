 LUMIÈRE Publication

By Indiefferential
August 25, 2026

LUMIÈRE by Indiefferential is a publication for faces, images and stories that deserve to be seen.

Rooted in modeling and photography, LUMIÈRE explores the space between fashion, beauty, portraiture and creative expression. We are interested in work that has a point of view, whether it is polished and fashion-forward, intimate and personal, raw and experimental, or simply impossible to look away from.

We welcome both emerging and established creatives. You do not need to fit a traditional definition of fashion to be considered. What matters is the image, the presence and the story behind it.

Who We Feature
  • Models
  • Editorials, model portfolios, beauty stories, portraits, personal features, digitals and creative series.Photographers
  • Fashion photography, portraiture, beauty, conceptual work, editorial series and experimental imagery.
  • Fashion Designers & Brands
  • Collections, lookbooks, campaigns, designer stories and fashion editorials.
  • Stylists & Beauty Creatives
  • Styling stories, hair and makeup editorials, beauty concepts and visual collaborations.
  • Creative Teams
  • Complete editorials bringing together models, photographers, stylists, makeup artists, designers and other creatives.
What LUMIÈRE Looks For

We are drawn to strong faces, distinctive imagery and a clear creative point of view.

A submission does not have to be extravagant to stand out. It can be minimal, cinematic, emotional, experimental, classic or raw.

We look for:

  • Strong visual identity
  • Thoughtful photography
  • Interesting models and personalities
  • Original concepts
  • Cohesive editorial series
  • Professional presentation
  • Clear creative direction
  • Complete and accurate credits

Above all, we look for work that feels like it belongs to someone — not something created simply to fill a page.

Publication Opportunities

Selected contributors may be considered for different LUMIÈRE feature formats depending on the project and editorial fit.

  • Print Editorials

Your work can be featured in a LUMIÈRE print issue as part of a curated editorial selection.

  • Covers

Particularly strong submissions may be considered for LUMIÈRE digital or print cover opportunities.

  • Model Features

Models can be presented through dedicated portfolio stories, beauty features, portraits or editorial series designed around their individual image and personality.

  • Interviews & Stories

Selected visual stories may be accompanied by interviews, biographies or editorial text exploring the person and the idea behind the work.

  • Portfolio Materials

Selected contributors may receive digital publication materials that can be used for portfolios, websites and social media.

  • Social Features

Selected stories may also be highlighted through LUMIÈRE's social media presence and digital channels.

Every publication is approached as an editorial piece — not simply as a collection of submitted images.

How to Submit

01 — Tell Us Who You Are

Introduce yourself and tell us what you do. Whether you are a model, photographer, stylist, designer or part of a creative team, we want to understand your role in the work.

02 — Show Us Your Work

Send your website, portfolio, Instagram or a selection of images that represents your current visual direction.

03 — Tell Us the Story

Give us some context about the project. What was the idea? Who was involved? What inspired it? Is it part of a larger series, portfolio or creative project?

You don't need to write an essay — just give us enough to understand the work.

04 — Editorial Selection

Our team reviews submissions based on visual quality, originality, concept, presentation and overall fit with LUMIÈRE.

Not every submission is selected, and selection does not depend solely on follower count, experience or commercial recognition.

05 — Publication

If your work is selected, you will receive the relevant contributor information and available publication options.

Once everything is confirmed, we collect the final images, credits and written information required to prepare the feature.

What to Send

To be considered for a LUMIÈRE feature, please send us the following:

  • Your full name
  • Creative role — model, photographer, stylist, designer, makeup artist, etc.
  • Instagram and/or website
  • City / country
  • A short introduction
  • Portfolio or selected images
  • Project or editorial title, if applicable
  • A short description of the concept or story
  • Full creative team credits
  • Any additional information you feel is relevant to the project

For editorials and collaborative projects, please include all participating creatives so that everyone involved can be properly credited.

Submit Your Work at editorial@indiefferential.com 

 

For Models

LUMIÈRE is particularly interested in discovering new faces.

You do not need to be represented by an agency or have an established editorial career to submit.

We welcome:

  • New faces
  • Established models
  • Independent models
  • Beauty models
  • Editorial models
  • Alternative and distinctive looks
  • Personal portrait series
  • Creative test shoots
  • Model-led editorial projects

Your submission can be a professional editorial, a carefully produced portfolio series or a collection of images that shows us who you are in front of the camera.

For Photographers

We want to see your eye.

LUMIÈRE is interested in photographers who create more than technically strong images — photographers with a recognizable way of seeing their subjects.

Fashion, portraiture, beauty, documentary-inspired work, conceptual photography and experimental imagery are all welcome.

A complete editorial is not required for every submission. A strong photographic series can be enough to start a conversation.

The LUMIÈRE Approach

We believe publication should feel meaningful.

LUMIÈRE is not designed to publish everything that is submitted to us. We curate the work that appears in our pages and digital spaces so that every feature contributes something to the identity of the publication.

We care about the model, the photographer, the image and the idea behind it.

We want to create a publication where emerging creatives can be discovered, established creatives can experiment, and strong images can exist beyond a social media feed.

If you have a face worth photographing, a story worth telling or an image worth remembering, we want to see it.

Submit your work to LUMIÈRE at editorial@indiefferential.com 

See you in the light.
