A Lifetime of Songs, and a Larger Idea of Legacy

Before a song becomes a recording, it can begin as a conviction about how people should remember one another. For ERICKOSCAR, that conviction reaches beyond the private dramas of a single life: a legacy worth leaving is one shaped by affection for other cultures and by people working together to make the world better. It is an expansive idea, but its roots are personal and musical.
