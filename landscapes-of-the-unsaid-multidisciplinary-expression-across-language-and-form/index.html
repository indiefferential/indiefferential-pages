Landscapes of the Unsaid: Multidisciplinary Expression across Language and Form

Founded in 2024 by Shanghai-born multi-hyphenate artist Yifei Zhou, the multidisciplinary project MIO-ERA serves as an expansive sanctuary for unspoken emotions, personal growth, and existential reflection. Functioning as primary songwriter, producer, vocalist, multi-instrumentalist, and visual designer, Zhou draws upon a diverse background encompassing traditional Chinese folk, shakuhachi music, Black American Music, classical, Brazilian styles, rock, electroacoustic experimentation, and noise.
