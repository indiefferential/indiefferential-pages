Through the Labyrinth: Qymira's Cinematic Leap From Stage to Screen

Global multimedia artist Qymira invites audiences into a spellbinding, cinematic universe with the release of the music video for "Labyrinth Of Life," out September 4th via QiiA Media, an ambitious, visually stunning production that transforms the song's themes of self-discovery and fate into a breathtaking adventure. Directed by acclaimed filmmaker Pedring Lopez, the video blurs the boundaries between music and cinema, opening inside an exclusive high-stakes poker game before reality fractures around Qymira, sweeping her through an ever-changing labyrinth of worlds spanning centuries and civilisations: dense jungles, ancient Egyptian battlefields, frozen kingdoms preparing for war, and cyberpunk cities glowing beneath neon skies. As Qymira has described it, every scene represents a different possibility, a different version of the self, and the video captures a long-held dream of making her music feel genuinely cinematic.
