Monsieur Herr Embraces the Absurdity of Making More Art on The Distortion Years

Monsieur Herr has arrived at an uncomfortable question: what justifies adding anything more to the noise? The Distortion Years, the latest album from the deliberately self-conscious project, approaches that dilemma with biting humour, restless experimentation and an exuberant disregard for conventional structure. It is an album that questions its own existence while enthusiastically making the case for it.
