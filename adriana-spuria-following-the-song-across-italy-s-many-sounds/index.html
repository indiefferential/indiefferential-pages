Adriana Spuria: Following the Song Across Italy’s Many Sounds

Adriana Spuria’s musical life began at seventeen, when singing and writing songs became part of a creative path that would cross genres, cities and roles. Over the years, she has moved through jazz, folk, blues, rock, funk and pop with different bands, while also working as a melody writer and vocalist on house and dance projects in the 1990s.
