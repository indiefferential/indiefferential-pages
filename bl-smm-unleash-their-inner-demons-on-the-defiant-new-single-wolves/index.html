BLØSMM Unleash Their Inner Demons on the Defiant New Single “Wolves”

Some battles are fought in the open; others take place in the darkest corners of the mind. On “Wolves,” Phoenix hard rock project BLØSMM confronts the fears, doubts and destructive inner voices that can gradually consume a person, turning an intensely personal struggle into a thunderous declaration of survival. Released November 13, 2026, the single pushes the band into darker, heavier territory, following the introduction of their sound with debut track “Poser.”
