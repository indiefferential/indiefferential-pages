A Love Letter to the Most High: Kings Blood Says "THANKS GOD"

Since he was a kid, Kings Blood dreamed of writing God a love letter. With his new single, "THANKS GOD," the Belgian rapper and singer has made that dream real. Written as a literal love letter to the Most High, the track is an uplifting, euphoric celebration of life, ancestry and divine timing, and it announces itself as a soulful and rhythmic testament to gratitude.
