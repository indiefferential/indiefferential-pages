Easy Thieves Turn Restless Nights and Open Roads Into Romantic Survival Rock

Somewhere between a restless night and an open highway lies the territory Easy Thieves have made their own. The Dallas outfit describes its sound as “Romantic Survival Rock,” a phrase that captures a blend of heartland rock, melodic alt-rock and indie emotional depth. Their songs move easily from intimate acoustic settings to full-throttle electric shows where the amp is cranked to ten, and the band’s work carries the lived-in quality of songs written by people who have been through something.
