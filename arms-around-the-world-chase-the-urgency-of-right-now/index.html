Arms Around the World Chase the Urgency of ‘Right Now’

“Right now” does not ask for patience. The first single from “Connection,” the forthcoming second album by arms around the world, pushes forward with a dark guitar drive, melody and lyric locked into rhythm. Jon Rimmerman wrote the song around hunger for everything at once, all the time; its repetition deliberately echoes his sense of 2026. A sustained, abrasive sound from his early-1970s Les Paul Gold Top gives the track its rough physical edge, while Hazel Stanfield’s backing harmonies lend the vocals a near-dreamlike beauty. Urgency and softness pull in opposite directions, and the friction is the point.
