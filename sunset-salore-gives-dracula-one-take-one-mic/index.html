Sunset Salore Gives 'Dracula' One Take, One Mic

Halloween has a new soundtrack, and it is played on a piano in a Melbourne studio with the lights low. Sunset Salore has released 'Dracula', a bold reinterpretation of the Tame Impala track, and the approach could hardly be more stripped back. The performance was recorded live as part of the 'one take Tuesdays' series at Humble Studios in Melbourne, with piano and vocals captured simultaneously on a single condenser mic while the whole thing was filmed on a phone with a gimbal.
