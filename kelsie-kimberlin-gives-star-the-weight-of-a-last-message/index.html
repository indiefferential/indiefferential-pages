Kelsie Kimberlin Gives ‘Star’ the Weight of a Last Message

“Star” carries a grief that begins with one unanswered message. Kelsie Kimberlin connects the song to “The Last Message,” her film about the death of her 21-year-old cousin in Ukraine and her journey there to ask forgiveness for not replying. The detail is painfully small beside the scale of loss, which is precisely why it holds such force. A message left unanswered becomes an emotional center, giving a song about mourning, memory and responsibility a specific human point of entry.
