Meghanne Storey Finds Renewal in the Spaces Between Genres

Wash Away begins with a history of uncertainty, but its musical character is anything but unsettled. The single, released July 24, 2026, grew from a song Brandt Parke wrote and composed roughly a decade earlier, during a difficult period marked by financial strain and personal uncertainty. When Parke brought the composition to Meghanne Storey, her immediate connection to its material opened the way to an intuitive collaboration. The song’s movement toward renewal thus passes through two distinct creative moments: its original writing and the later encounter that gave it a new voice.
