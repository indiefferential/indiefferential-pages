Electrick Reckoning Turns Political Disillusionment Into Funky, Furious Rock on "S.O.S."

Asong about banking might not sound like the obvious foundation for a hard hitting rock track, but Electrick Reckoning has never seemed particularly interested in obvious choices. With "S.O.S. (Same Old Shit)", Ken Woods and his band take political disappointment, economic frustration and years of accumulated disillusionment and turn them into a restless piece of music that refuses to sit quietly in the background.
