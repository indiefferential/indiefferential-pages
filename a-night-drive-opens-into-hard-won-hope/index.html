A Night Drive Opens Into Hard-Won Hope

Hi Ho, Six Shooter! have always known how to put a story in motion. Long Mountain Road begins with a solitary night drive and expands into a meditation on time, loss, and love, its movement toward hope earned rather than declared. The single, released October 16, 2026, brings the Virginia band’s familiar twang into a more cinematic frame: reverb-drenched Telecaster and lap steel meet a soaring string quartet, widening the emotional horizon without abandoning the group’s roots.
