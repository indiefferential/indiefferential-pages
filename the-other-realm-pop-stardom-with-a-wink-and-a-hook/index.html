The Other Realm: Pop Stardom, With a Wink and a Hook

The Other Realm’s “Wannabe Popstar” understands that the dream of fame is both seductive and ridiculous. The New Haven artist wants the spectacle—screaming fans, elaborate stages and the status of being somebody—while keeping enough distance to laugh at the fantasy. That tension gives the fourth album its comic engine. It celebrates pop’s hooks, drama and theatrical self-importance even as it satirizes the gap between a glamorous vision of stardom and the less glamorous reality of making independent music.
