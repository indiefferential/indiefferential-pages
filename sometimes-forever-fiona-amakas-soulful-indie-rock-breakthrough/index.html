Sometimes Forever: Fiona Amaka's Soulful Indie-Rock Breakthrough

Fiona Amaka's new single, "Sometimes Forever," leans into folk-rock, Americana and classic rock influences, and has already been described by listeners and social media followers as catchy, exciting and possibly her best work yet, feedback that marks an encouraging moment for the London-based songwriter as she continues building her catalogue.
