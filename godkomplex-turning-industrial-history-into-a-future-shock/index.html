Godkomplex: Turning Industrial History into a Future Shock

Godkomplex began in 1998 with an intention that still defines the project: to revitalize hard industrial music without losing the force of its earlier forms. Founded by Mr. Panik and Download, the duo has built a sound that pulls from drum and bass, power noise, techno, trance and coldwave. Those ingredients do not settle into a smooth hybrid.
