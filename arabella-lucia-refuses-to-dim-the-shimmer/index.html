Arabella Lucia Refuses to Dim the ‘Shimmer’

Arabella Lucia’s guitar does not arrive as decoration. It is part of the argument. On “Steal My Shimmer,” the London-based Armenian-Canadian singer, guitarist and songwriter turns a bruising encounter into a bright, pointed refusal to shrink. A male musician questioned whether her Gibson SG belonged to her father and made passive-aggressive attempts to undermine her playing. Lucia did not internalize the slight; she made it the engine of a song about talent, sexism and the quiet pressure to make other people comfortable.
