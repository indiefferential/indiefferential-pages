Zhenyok Chem Brat Make Positivity a Full-Contact Sport

Zhenyok Chem Brat treats genre as a meeting place. The Napa-based project combines DJ-producer Zhenyok, also known as Jeff Craft, with drummer Chango, bringing rock, funk, electronic dance music and dubstep into a sound shaped by live performance and studio craft. Their single “Lay Your Heart Down,” released October 30, 2026, continues a mission that reaches beyond style: making music with meaningful messages, using humor and positivity to bring people together.
