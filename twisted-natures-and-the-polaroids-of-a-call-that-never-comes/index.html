Twisted Natures and the Polaroids of a Call That Never Comes

The hardest part of a breakup is rarely the day it happens. It is the long aftermath, when a scent in a stairwell or a late-night habit pulls someone back into a room that no longer exists. Twisted Natures builds an entire song inside that aftermath. 'Do You Think of Me Too?' is a nostalgic alternative electronic-pop track about the memories that linger after a relationship ends, and about the quiet question that sits at its center: if one person still thinks of the other, does the feeling run both ways?
