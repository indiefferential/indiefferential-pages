Saša Miočić: Bringing Cinematic Metal Out of the Machine

Saša Miočić’s music starts from a striking collision: the elegance of piano and strings set against the weight of heavy guitar and industrial percussion. The Croatian composer, producer, live sound engineer and multi-instrumentalist works at the meeting point of neoclassical writing, neo-metal and cinematic production. “Take What You Need,” a fifteen-track solo album released through Platforma Z Records, gives that synthesis room to unfold across an expansive instrumental landscape.
