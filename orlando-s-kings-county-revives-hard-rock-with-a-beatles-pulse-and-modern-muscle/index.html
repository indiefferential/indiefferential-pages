Orlando’s Kings County Revives Hard Rock With a Beatles Pulse and Modern Muscle

Orlando has no shortage of rock bands with big ambitions, but Kings County carries its ambition in the sound itself. The band makes hard-hitting rock built on infectious hooks, and it does so with a sense of purpose that is clear from the first riff. Its music blends aggressive guitar work with powerful melodic vocals, creating a mix that sits between the raw energy of 1990s hard rock and the sharper, heavier edge of today’s modern hard-hitters.
