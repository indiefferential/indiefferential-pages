Marie Nelson Wraps a Real Love Story in ‘Deck My Halls’

Marie Nelson’s “Deck My Halls” begins with a seasonal assignment and turns into something more personal. Invited to perform for a Baby Blue Arts holiday television program, the Minnesota singer-songwriter chose not to reach for another familiar standard. She wrote an original Christmas song instead, shaping a playful, slightly cheeky love story from the sounds she loves: the rock-and-roll snap of Chuck Berry’s “Run Rudolph Run,” the holiday glow associated with The Ronettes and the expansive pop warmth of Mariah Carey.
