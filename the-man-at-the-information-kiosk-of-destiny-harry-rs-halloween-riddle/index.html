The Man at the Information Kiosk of Destiny: Harry R's Halloween Riddle

Not every artist bio reads like straightforward promotional copy, and The Harry R Pi's latest offering makes no attempt to pretend otherwise. Described simply as a Halloween-inspired instrumental, best enjoyed while dancing around in a skeleton onesie, the track arrives wrapped in a persona that is part riddle, part philosophy lecture. Harry R, as the project frames him, is a guide, a thought without a thinker, a guide through the dark, a number that can never be counted. The song itself is existential in many ways, built with layers deliberately left to be discovered only upon further listening.
