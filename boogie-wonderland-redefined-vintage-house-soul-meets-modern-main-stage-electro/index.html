Boogie Wonderland Redefined: Vintage House Soul Meets Modern Main Stage Electro

Formed in 2024 following the release of their debut album Eerste, London-based dance-pop duo Junonuno—comprising producer DJ Juno and vocalist Nuno—has rapidly carved out a distinct identity by fusing nineties house soul with contemporary pop sensibilities. Fresh from a landmark performance on the Main Stage at Nowadaze Festival and a sold-out debut of their hybrid DJ and live vocal setup in Soho, the pair continues to expand their clubbing footprint.
