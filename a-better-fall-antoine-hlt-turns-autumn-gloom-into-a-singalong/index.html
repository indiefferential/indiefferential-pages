A Better Fall: Antoine HLT Turns Autumn Gloom Into a Singalong

Every year, the same thing happens to Antoine HLT: the fall goes badly. Rather than feeling sorry for himself, the French singer-songwriter did what he usually does and turned it into a song, one he admits exaggerates the lyrics just a tiny bit. The result is "Vive l'automne", a humorous and unifying autumn anthem, accompanied by a delightful music video, and it arrives with a promise that this fall, at least, is sure to be better.
