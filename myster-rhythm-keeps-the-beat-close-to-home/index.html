Myster Rhythm Keeps the Beat Close to Home

For Myster Rhythm, a song becomes real where the beat drops and the pen meets the page. That straightforward creative philosophy underpins Cutie, an original single recorded in the artist’s East Tennessee hometown and released October 31, 2025. Featuring CJay and produced by Justin Jay Beats, the track pairs a simple starting point with a process that can become more complex once the writing begins.
