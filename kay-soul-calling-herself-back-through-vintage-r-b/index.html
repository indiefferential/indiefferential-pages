Kay Soul: Calling Herself Back Through Vintage R&B

Kay Soul’s “Anybody Out There” is both a reaching question and an act of self-reclamation. The single looks toward the aftermath of toxic love, when a person can lose sight of herself inside a relationship and must find a way back. Its title carries the loneliness of that search, but the song’s emotional movement does not stop at asking who might answer.
