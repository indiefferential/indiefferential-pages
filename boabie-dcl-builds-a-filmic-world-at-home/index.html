Boabie-DCL Builds a Filmic World at Home

Boabie-DCL’s music begins in a home studio, but its sense of scale reaches beyond the room. The Glasgow-based electronic artist draws on cinematic composer Hans Zimmer, working with melodies, electronica, and rhythmic beats to create productions that balance simplicity with emotional intensity. That combination runs through the account of SuperStatusEvent, a single made entirely in the same independent environment where the artist can shape each layer himself.
