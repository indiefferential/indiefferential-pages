23 Fields Looks Back Without Losing the Road Ahead

The distance between seventeen and middle age can feel like a road whose turns only become visible in retrospect. 23 Fields makes that distance the emotional ground of a latest offering, looking back at an earlier self and considering where the journey has led. The premise is simple, but it opens onto a large subject: how a person changes while the memories that formed them remain close enough to touch.
