Zachary Mason and the Quiet Art of Making Hundreds of Beginnings

Zachary Mason’s creative life is measured not only in finished releases but in the steady accumulation of possibilities. Since spring 2021, the Guildford-based artist has made between 200 and 250 demo tracks, working with a recording kit gifted by a relative and purchased from Andertons Music, located just across the road from his residence. That modestly local detail sits beside a broad output: a musician building songs in a home setting, across genres, with acoustic and electric guitars as well as keyboards.
