The Luca Yeager Band Question the Cost of Being Right on "Right or Wrong"

Being right can become a pursuit of its own, sometimes so consuming that the original point of an argument disappears. The need to win takes priority over understanding, and the satisfaction of proving a point can come at the expense of something far more valuable. On "Right or Wrong," their latest single, The Luca Yeager Band turn that familiar conflict into a driving rock song about perspective, priorities and the things people risk losing when they care too much about having the final word.
