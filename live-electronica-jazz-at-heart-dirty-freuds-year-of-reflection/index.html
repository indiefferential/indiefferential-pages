Live Electronica, Jazz at Heart: Dirty Freud's Year of Reflection

Dirty Freud is back with a new single, "We Do What We Do," the first of two singles set to arrive ahead of the forthcoming EP Under The Influence. It caps what has been a year of reflection, one spent thinking carefully about how his sound has been built over time, and the new track charts a clear direction in how that sound has developed across the years.
