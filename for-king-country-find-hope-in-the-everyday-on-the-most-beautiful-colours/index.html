for KING + COUNTRY Find Hope in the Everyday on "the most beautiful colours"

After years of touring, recording and performing for audiences around the world, Joel and Luke Smallbone arrived at an unexpected conclusion. They needed to stop. For a duo whose career has been defined by movement, packed arenas and an ever expanding global audience, stepping away from the noise created the space for something quieter and, ultimately, more meaningful. That pause became the foundation for "the most beautiful colours", the fifth studio album from four time GRAMMY Award winners for KING + COUNTRY.
