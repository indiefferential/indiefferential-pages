An Honest Look Into the Mirror: The Night and The Dirty Reflect

Taking stock of one's own faults is never easy. A love lost, the passing of a dear friend, life changing course, regrets and untruths all come crowding in. "Reflections," the new single from Seattle's The Night and The Dirty, is an honest look into the mirror, and it arrives on October 13 across major streaming platforms. The band combines its folk-tinged, synth-colored "Surf Drench" sound into what it presents as a much-needed emotional release.
