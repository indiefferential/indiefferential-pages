EYE OF TJ and the Holiday Song That Makes Room for Grief

EYE OF TJ makes room for the feeling that holiday music often leaves outside the door. Christmas Doesn’t Feel the Same, scheduled for release on November 20, 2026, begins from a familiar December scene: a place at the holiday table where someone dearly missed should be. Rather than disguise that absence with seasonal brightness, the song treats grief and gratitude as emotions able to occupy the same room. Its promise is not easy cheer, but recognition, and that distinction gives the forthcoming single its quiet force.
