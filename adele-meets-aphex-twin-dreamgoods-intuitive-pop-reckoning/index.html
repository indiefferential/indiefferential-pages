Adele Meets Aphex Twin: DreamGood's Intuitive Pop Reckoning

DreamGood have never been shy about the strangeness of their own sound, and their new single "Lifeline" puts that strangeness front and center. Described by the band as a soft-boy introvert's anthem about believing in your own innate intuition, the track has been compared to Adele commingling with Aphex Twin, an electronic, weirdo alt-pop offering aimed squarely at fans of Perfume Genius, Four Tet, Belinda Carlisle and George Michael. "Lifeline" marks the second single from the group's forthcoming concept album, Paris Novella (Fortune Teller), a project built to explore a kaleidoscope of emotion and ideas while inviting listeners to think, dance and flex in the mirror right alongside it.
