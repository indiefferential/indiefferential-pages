Robert Silvester Lets the Sunshine Find Its Groove

Some songs arrive with a concept; Beautiful Day began with a walk. Robert Silvester says its lyrics came spontaneously while he was outside on a sunny day with headphones on, a small moment of unplanned attention that became the seed for a polished single. The eventual recording took roughly ten attempts to get right. That process matters because the track’s ease is not accidental: its relaxed surface is the outcome of repeated experimentation, with Silvester continuing to adjust the sound until it felt right.
