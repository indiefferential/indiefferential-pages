After the Movies: Turning Family Memory into Slowcore

“stars.” begins with a fear too large for a grand gesture: the knowledge that someone loved will not be here forever. After the Movies, a Norwegian slowcore project formed by childhood friends Morten Samdal and Thomas Ingdal, approach that knowledge through fragile melody, rough edges and space. The debut single moves between slowcore, lo-fi indie and alternative rock, allowing unease to linger instead of forcing it into a dramatic resolution.
