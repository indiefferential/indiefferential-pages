Six Years, One Sanity: Lucky Duck's Genre-Defying Memoir

Lucky Duck, hailing from Ardsley, United States, is making waves in the music scene with an approach that refuses easy categorization. Collaborating with Grammy winner Denise Barbarita for production and mixing, as well as Grammy-nominated engineer Chris Allgood for mastering, Lucky Duck's music showcases genuinely top-tier talent behind the scenes. Influenced by artists including Boygenius, Clairo and beabadoobee, alongside SZA, Tinashe, Daft Punk, Kendrick Lamar and Sam Hunt, Lucky Duck has crafted a sonic identity built on a blend of emotional depth and raw authenticity, one that resonates across an unusually wide range of listeners.
