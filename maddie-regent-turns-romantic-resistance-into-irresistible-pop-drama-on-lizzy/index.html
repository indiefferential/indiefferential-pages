Maddie Regent Turns Romantic Resistance Into Irresistible Pop Drama on “Lizzy”

Some love stories begin with a spark. Others begin with an argument, a stubborn refusal to admit attraction, and the increasingly desperate realisation that staying away is no longer an option. On “Lizzy”, her latest single, Los Angeles-based singer-songwriter Maddie Regent leans into the delicious contradiction of wanting someone while insisting they are entirely the wrong person. Playful, cinematic and charged with romantic tension, the track unfolds like an enemies-to-lovers story in which denial becomes just another expression of desire.
