Yin, Yang and the Dancefloor: FREDRYD's "The Sun And The Moon"

Night out or day out, sun or moon, the differences between people matter less than the fun they can have together. That is the idea behind "The Sun And The Moon," the new and final single from FREDRYD's upcoming album. FREDRYD is a Swedish indie producer and DJ and a former dancer, making music about life enjoyment, girl power, social connection and reconnection, and fun, and describing himself as both a feminist and an optimist.
