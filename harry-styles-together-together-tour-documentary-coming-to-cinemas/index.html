Harry Styles 'Together, Together' Tour Documentary Coming to Cinemas

A documentary about Harry Styles' 'Together, Together' tour is in the works, according to reports from Variety. The film will feature footage of Styles' New York residency and highlight authentic human stories involved in the tour, including the singer and his fans as well as those behind the scenes. John Wilson, director of films such as 'The History Of Concrete' and HBO's 'How To With John Wilson,' is set to direct this documentary which will be a hybrid of live gig footage and other content.

The film, scheduled for theatrical release, is expected to cover Styles' extensive tour that began in May. The 82-date run included shows at London's Wembley Stadium and New York's Madison Square Garden, among others. Styles announced the tour extension recently with final dates set for July, August, September, and October.

The documentary will also support Green Nation – Live Nation’s global sustainability initiative – to reduce emissions and promote lower-impact travel options for fans where available. In London, proceeds from ticket sales will be donated to LIVE Trust, which helps protect grassroots venues and talent, while in the US, they'll support HeadCount.

In recent sightings, John Wilson has been seen filming Styles while out and about in New York. The documentary is expected to be a hybrid of live gig footage and other content.
