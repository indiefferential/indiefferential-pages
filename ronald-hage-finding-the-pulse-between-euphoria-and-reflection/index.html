Ronald Hage: Finding the Pulse Between Euphoria and Reflection

Ronald Hage’s melodic house takes shape at an acoustic piano, where a melody can emerge before drums and synthesizers begin to define its destination. That process gives “Doesn’t Get Better Than This” a human starting point, even as the finished single is designed for the energy of the dancefloor. Hage, a producer from The Hague, works between melodic house and progressive house, combining electronic atmosphere with strong, memorable lines.
