ALEN HIT Reopens the Story With a Funk-Pop Pulse

A comeback can be framed as a return to the past, but ALEN HIT’s next chapter is built around a new set of stories. The Belarus-born, Dubai-based singer, songwriter, and composer is preparing Traveler’s Notes, a ten-track concept album shaped over nearly four years in Dubai. Its ten songs are described as personal chapters exploring love, freedom, loss, loneliness, self-discovery, and the courage to start again.
