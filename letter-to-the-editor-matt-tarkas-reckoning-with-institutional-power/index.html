Letter to the Editor: Matt Tarka's Reckoning With Institutional Power

Since 2011, Lancaster, Pennsylvania-based singer-songwriter Matt Tarka has built a reputation for fearless social commentary and poignant observations of power imbalances within American society, self-releasing a compelling catalogue of EPs including Motorcycle Breakfast, Surely Late and Vision Hazy, alongside singles that consistently demonstrate his commitment to addressing the pressing issues of the moment. His pandemic-era releases "Good Morning, Sympathy" and "Crowds" drew attention for their prescient documentation of turbulent times, establishing Tarka as a habitual note-taker of a genuinely tumultuous era.
