Lived-In and Unfiltered: DedFACE's Trap Confessions From Riga

Some artists build a persona to hide behind. DedFACE has built his entire sound around refusing to. Dmitrijs Vasenovs, better known by that name, is an independent artist from Riga, Latvia, who since his 2023 debut has carved out a distinctive niche by blending raw honesty with genuine emotional volatility rather than smoothing either into something more comfortable to package.
