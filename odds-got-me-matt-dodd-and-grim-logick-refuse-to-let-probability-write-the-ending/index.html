Odds Got Me: MaTT DoDD and Grim Logick Refuse to Let Probability Write the Ending

Released May 29, 2026, "Odds Got Me (feat. Grim Logick)" is an advance single from MaTT DoDD's forthcoming FULL FORCE 2, a record built around one central idea: overwhelming odds do not get to determine the outcome. MaTT, who has continued creating for more than thirteen years while living with Muscular Dystrophy, established the song's million-to-one premise through his own production and opening verse. Grim Logick answers that perspective from a different angle, addressing recovery, fatherhood and rebuilding after years of instability, turning the track into a genuine lyrical conversation rather than a conventional feature spot.
