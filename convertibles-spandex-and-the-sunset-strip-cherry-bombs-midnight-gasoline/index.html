Convertibles, Spandex and the Sunset Strip: Cherry Bomb's Midnight Gasoline

Some bands treat the 1980s as a reference point. Cherry Bomb treats them as a home address. Midnight Gasoline is the group's third album, following Pandora's Box in 2022 and Sunset BLVD in 2024, which keeps to a rhythm of releasing an album every two years. Work on the new record began in 2025 and sticks to the same pattern for upbeat songs: get in your convertible, turn up the volume and enjoy the show.
