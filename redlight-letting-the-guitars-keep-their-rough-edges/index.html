redLight: Letting the Guitars Keep Their Rough Edges

RedLight’s “Ten Circles” puts guitars at the front and leaves their rough edges intact. The indie-rock single is built around the pleasure of playing: punchy riffs, gritty textures, a driving rhythm and small musical details that keep the arrangement in motion. Rather than smoothing the song into a highly polished surface, RedLight lets the band sound breathe, preserving the spontaneous character that comes from musicians working together.
