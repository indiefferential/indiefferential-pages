TJ Jones: Finding Hope in the Waiting Between Plans

“Grand Plans” is built for the uncertain stretch when life refuses to explain itself. TJ Jones’s faith-rooted song speaks to waiting, disappointment, change and the unease of not knowing what comes next. Its central reassurance is not that every setback will make sense immediately, but that peace can be found in trusting that God’s plans exceed what a person can see or arrange alone.
