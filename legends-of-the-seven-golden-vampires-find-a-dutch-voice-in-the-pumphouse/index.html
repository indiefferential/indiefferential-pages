Legends of the Seven Golden Vampires Find a Dutch Voice in the Pumphouse

Some bands announce a change of direction with a press release. Legends of the Seven Golden Vampires announce theirs with a new voice. The Hertfordshire dream pop band returns with The Pumphouse of Broken Dreams, the follow-up to Psychedelic Soul & Trash Vol.3, and the new record tightens the focus of a group once admired, and occasionally questioned, for refusing to stay in one place.
