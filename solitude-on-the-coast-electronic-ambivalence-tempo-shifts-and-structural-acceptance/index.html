Solitude on the Coast: Electronic Ambivalence, Tempo Shifts, and Structural Acceptance

Los Angeles-based electronic producer OKHABIN, born in South Korea and trained in computer engineering at Columbia University, crafts meticulously structured pop-influenced electronic music rooted in deep emotional reflection. Transitioning to full-time music after a chance encounter with M83’s Anthony Gonzalez, OKHABIN spent two and a half years self-producing his debut album It’s a Dance, set for release on December 5th, 2026.
