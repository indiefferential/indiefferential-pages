Dan Drive - Tweedy MKII - Overdrive

Thierry L'Écuyer December 9, 2021 Pedal Reviews

That Tweed Amp Goodness, in pedal form. I was super lucky to find one of these and it did not disappoint. Very responsive and dynamic, but also brings that grit drive we all know and love. The @dandrivepedal Tweedy simply rocks!🔥

The TWEEDY from DanDrive is inspired by the Deluxe 5B3, giving you access to the feel and response of the classic circuit without the hassle. It enhances the tone of your guitar and the way you play, adding that Deluxe sound to your signal. Simple, clean control layout coupled with the lacquered tweed cover.

To capture this tone and behavior was our goal! The old Tweed feel.
