SKAGS Turn Digital Paralysis Into Cinematic Prog Rock on Inertia / Ad Nauseam

SKAGS: The Rock band you need on your playlist, comes from Greece! Its review of the band’s latest album gave it a 9/10 and went further, declaring that it is criminal to ignore them. The Athens alternative and progressive rock outfit has earned that praise with Inertia / Ad Nauseam, its third concept album, a dark and cinematic journey into modern digital paralysis and dystopian consumerism. The praise is a strong endorsement from a publication focused on heavy music.
