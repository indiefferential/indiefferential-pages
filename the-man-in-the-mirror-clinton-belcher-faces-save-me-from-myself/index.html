The Man in the Mirror: Clinton Belcher Faces "Save Me From Myself"

Some battles never show up on the outside. Independent country artist and songwriter Clinton Belcher builds his new single, "Save Me From Myself," around exactly that kind of struggle: the fight nobody else can see, and the uncomfortable realization that sometimes the hardest person to overcome is yourself. It is an emotionally raw country song, and it arrives with no interest in dressing the subject up.
