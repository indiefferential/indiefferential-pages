The Moonlit Walk Finds Renewal in the Lessons of the Past on "Better Times"

Moving forward does not always mean leaving the past behind. Sometimes, it means learning to carry its lessons differently, recognizing both the difficulties and the moments of happiness that have shaped the person we become. That balance between gratitude and renewal sits at the heart of "Better Times," the debut single from The Moonlit Walk, the authorial project of composer Gianfranco Cacciola, released on September 25, 2026.
