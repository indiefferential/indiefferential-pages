Sara Diana Turns Attraction Into a Cinematic Obsession on "Her Hair's Like Fire"

Some people leave an impression before a single word has been exchanged. Their presence seems to alter the atmosphere, drawing attention with an intensity that is difficult to explain and even harder to ignore. On "Her Hair's Like Fire," her latest single, 20 year old singer songwriter Sara Diana explores that magnetic pull, turning attraction, mystery and obsession into a vivid pop narrative shaped by striking imagery and emotionally charged vocals.
