Nervous Bride Find Their Most Vulnerable Moment in the Dreamlike “We Had a Dream About the Ocean”

A shared dream can linger long after the person who once shared it is gone. It is the kind of memory that returns without warning, turning an ordinary moment into something quietly devastating. On “We Had a Dream About the Ocean,” Austin musician and producer Bruce William Ewing, the creative force behind Nervous Bride, explores that emotional aftershock with unusual openness. Released November 13, the single is the project's most ambitious production to date, but also its most unguarded: a portrait of someone alone in a hotel room, wide awake, remembering a dream that once belonged to two people.
